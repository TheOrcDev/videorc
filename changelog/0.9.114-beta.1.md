## Settings in tabs

Settings is split into seven tabs across the top: General, Recording,
Permissions, Shortcuts, Remote, Orcle and About. Pick one and only that part
of Settings shows, and the tabs stay in place while you scroll. The
Shortcuts tab puts your global shortcuts next to every keyboard shortcut in
Videorc.

Settings remembers the tab you used last. Search (⌘K) lists every tab, and
the update notice in the sidebar, the FFmpeg warning in Studio and the
message about a shortcut that could not be registered each open the tab
that fixes them.

**Animate scene changes** is now under General.
