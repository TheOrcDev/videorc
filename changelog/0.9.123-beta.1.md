## Live captions on vertical streams

When you streamed to a vertical destination (YouTube Shorts, TikTok,
Instagram) at the same time as a horizontal one, live captions could show in
the app and never reach the video. Turning captions on after you went live
had the same gap.

Captions now burn on both streams. The vertical captions are sized for a
phone screen and sit clear of the TikTok, Shorts and Reels button bands. The
recording also carries the captions while a vertical destination is on, so
you do not get a second captioned copy. The Captions controls say so.

## Toasts stay clear

A warning or error toast used to wash the whole panel with amber or red.
Every toast is the same glass as the rest of the app. Success, warning, error
and info only colour the 16 px icon.
