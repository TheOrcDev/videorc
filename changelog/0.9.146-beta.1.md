## Discord go-live posts

In Livestream → Setup, open Discord and paste a webhook URL for the channel you
want. While Announce when I go live is on, every public Go Live posts one
message there under your name and avatar, with the stream title, the thumbnail
and a link button for each destination that's live. Unlisted and private
streams never post.

The post gains buttons as more destinations come up, and when you stop it turns
into "Was live" with how long you streamed. Pick No ping, @here or @everyone;
the default is No ping. You can turn off the small "Live with Videorc" footer.
