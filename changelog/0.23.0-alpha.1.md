## New

- **Stream Manager Screen key** for the Stream Deck: tap it to move the Stream Manager between your Presenting and Monitor screens, or hold it to show the Stream Manager on the other screen until you let go. The app you're presenting keeps the focus. On Linux, taps work on X11; on Wayland the key reads Not on Wayland and a hold still works. If your Videorc is too old for the key, it reads Update Videorc. Thanks @franky47! (#1307)

## Fixes

- The Scene canvas shows the layout, not the live picture, and now says so under the canvas, with a button to open, show or pop out the Preview where the picture is. (#1301)

## Windows

- Switching scenes with Clips or a recording on no longer shows "Couldn't confirm the scene change". The switch is confirmed once the recording and the Preview draw the new scene. (#1288)
