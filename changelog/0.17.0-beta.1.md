## Scenes

- New scene… lists your open apps with a picture of each front window and
  makes one scene per app. A scene follows its app's front window and comes
  back when you quit and relaunch the app.
- Switching scenes changes the capture in place and holds the last frame
  until the new window's first frame, so nothing flashes black.
- The Scene page lists your saved scenes, then the built-in layouts. Drag to
  reorder. Keys, Stream Deck, the phone and agents can switch scenes.

## Hide screen

- Press Control-Option-H, or use the button on Studio's Screen row, the
  Stream Manager or the command menu, to turn your screen into a frosted
  still on the recording, every stream, the Screen file and Clips.

## Recording

- Record refuses a recordings folder it can't write to before it starts,
  instead of failing a moment later.
- When the disk fills during a take, Videorc says so plainly and keeps what
  was recorded. The low-space notice now shows when you press Record.
- A Record start that was refused shows as "Didn't start" in the Library,
  not as an empty MKV.
- Move to Trash keeps the recording's file name in the Trash.

## Studio

- The Chat section appearing on air no longer shrinks the preview card.
- Settings → Shortcuts shows Space in Studio for Record, instead of
  Unassigned.

## Go Live

- The performance check runs with the Clips ring on, so Check this computer
  works and an untouched stream can step down when the computer can't keep
  up.

## Look

- The Liquid Chrome ground is a still again, with no drift.
