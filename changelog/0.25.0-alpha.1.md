## New

- **HTML banners on Windows and Linux**: Assets → Banners → **Import HTML…** turns an `.html` file, or a folder with `index.html` and its images, fonts, styles and scripts, into a banner with a transparent background that can move, in the Preview, the recording and the stream. It works like any other banner. (#1326)
- **Stream watchdog**: while you're live, Videorc watches what viewers get. When the mix has been silent for 20 seconds ("Viewers hear nothing"), the picture stops, a platform stays dark, YouTube reports no audio, or several people in chat say they can't hear you, a card in the Studio and the Stream Manager says so with a one-tap fix like Unmute, End takeover or Reconnect, plus a system notification. The Library's session details list every alert afterwards. (#727)
- **Community**: Videorc remembers the people in your chat on this computer. Chat rows show "Regular" and "Back" chips, Buddy welcomes regulars and comebacks and celebrates milestones like a 10th stream, and Reports adds a Community block. Viewers can join their accounts across platforms with `!link`, undo it with `!unlink`, and delete themselves with `!forgetme`. (#727)

## Changes

- The status bar no longer shows the "Clip" timer, which counted up even with nothing recording. The Clips ring still keeps the last minute in Output → Clips. (#1332)
