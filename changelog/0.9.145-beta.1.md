## Separate source recordings

Turn on Separate source recordings in Output → Local recording. Each recording
then also saves a Screen file, the screen or window you captured with the
system audio track, and a Camera file with the microphone track, beside the
Combined recording and at the same size. Put them on separate tracks in your
editor and move, crop or mix them however you like. A source that's off
records as silence.

The preview, the Combined recording and your streams stay composed exactly as
before. The switch needs local recording, a camera and a screen or window;
without them the session records the Combined file only, and the setting tells
you why. It's off by default and free for everyone.

Writing three 4K files at once is heavy. If the Screen or Camera file stutters,
record at a smaller size.

## Library

The files from one recording show together, each labelled Screen, Camera or
Combined.

## Stream Manager

The stats bar no longer shows the stream health dot and bitrate. Session time,
viewers and followers keep their places, and a failed destination still shows
in Activity.
