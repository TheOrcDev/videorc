This is the next Windows and Linux Alpha after 0.10.1-alpha.1.

## Five more live backgrounds

Assets → Backgrounds now has ten live backgrounds. The five new ones are
Beams, Color Bends, Dither, Ferrofluid and Ghost Fibers, from React Bits by
David Haz.

Each one has three looks. Click a tile to apply it, then use Adjust style to
change its colours and motion. The same background shows in your preview, in
your recording and on your stream. They are free for everyone.

If your computer is struggling, Videorc draws a live background at a smaller
size first, then falls back to a still poster, so your recording keeps its
frame rate.

## Windows fix

On Windows, a slow microphone no longer makes Record fail. Videorc now gives
a slow microphone more time to start, so your recording starts normally with
live microphone switching and system audio, and the Record button no longer
shows a confusing busy message while it waits.

## Level meter colours

The LCD level meter now turns amber and then red as your level gets hot, and
the held peak, the digits and the CLIP flag follow, so a loud microphone no
longer looks like a quiet one. It also sits on the app's glass, and Studio no
longer shows dB numbers.
