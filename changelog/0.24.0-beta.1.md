## New

- **HTML banners**: Assets → Banners has **Import HTML…** beside Import image…. Import an `.html` file, or a folder with `index.html` and its images, fonts, styles and scripts, and it becomes a banner with a transparent background that can move. It works like any other banner: Show and Hide, its place on the Scene page, opacity, Stream or Recording, the Stream Manager, the Stream Deck, the phone and Buddy. (#1323)
- **A check before going live Unlisted or Private**: when a YouTube destination would go out Unlisted or Private, Go live asks first and names it, with a button to change the privacy. The Privacy field now shows the privacy that is actually sent. (#1316)
- **A slow scene switch keeps its name**: the scene you clicked keeps its name with "Applying…", and after a moment a line says what it's waiting for, like the camera's first frame. A new click takes over the switch. Thanks @petercr! (#1312)

## Fixes

- YouTube chat waits for YouTube to open a new broadcast's chat instead of failing for the whole stream. (#1317)
- Live captions turned on mid-stream now reach the stream, and the Live captions switch says when transcription is paused on our side. (#1315)
- "Buddy, say hello to Jay in the chat" now gets a greeting in Buddy's bubble on the stream. (#1318)
- An animated live background starts moving again by itself after it was stepped down to a still frame, for example after a takeover screen. (#1322)
