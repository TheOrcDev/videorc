## Auto-show Activity on stream

The Stream Manager's Activity pane has a new **Auto-show** switch. When it's
on, every new follow, subscription, membership, gift, tip, raid and watch
streak shows up on your stream as the same glass highlight card you get from
"Show on stream", so viewers are thanked on screen without you touching the
window.

Auto-show is off by default. It never replaces a card that's already showing:
when several events arrive at once it shows the newest few one after another
and skips stale ones.
