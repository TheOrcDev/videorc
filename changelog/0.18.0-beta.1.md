## New

- Camera look: brightness, contrast and saturation for your camera.
- Continuity Camera is never lost or taken.
- Crop the screen, Fill for landscape, and Screen size on the Scene page.
- A stuck Preview lives inside the main window, and a docked Preview never
  brings up a second, empty Preview window.
- A Chat row in the Studio inspector.
- Displays you can tell apart, that survive hot-plug and resolution changes.
- Bigger hit targets; the destination row opens from its chevron.
- Pages fit a small window.
- X chat with a stream key says how to get it.

## Fixes

- A missing screen or camera never rewrites your choice; window and display
  names stay apart, and your choice comes back after a scene change.
- The Studio reconnects when the engine drops its socket.
- A vertical destination the raw encoder path can't stream needs your
  attention before Go live.
- A refused MP4 export says it needs disk space.
- Detached windows left open at quit open again.
- Proof-surface layers stack in scene order.
- The phone remote's Record LED matches the desktop again.

## Under the hood

- Main CI repaired, the Windows clippy wall burned down, and smoke and docs
  fixes.
