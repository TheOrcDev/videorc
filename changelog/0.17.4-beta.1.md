## Improvements

- Record is one metallic red button.

## Fixes

- The "Saving MP4" toast no longer sticks at 98% after the MP4 is ready, and
  "MP4 ready" no longer says long recordings take minutes to save.
- Your display stays awake while a recording or livestream runs.
- A Preview left open at quit opens again at the next launch.
- No "Videorc hit a problem" toast while the first-run performance check runs.
- In Your look, device chips truncate long names instead of clipping them.

## Under the hood

- Backend unit tests never write the developer's real secret store, plus
  test-loop and docs fixes.
