This is the first Linux Alpha **candidate** for Videorc **0.9.109**. It is
for internal validation on Ubuntu 24.04 before any public Linux Alpha
program opens.

## What this build is for

- Confirm the AppImage starts, captures display and camera through PipeWire
  and the desktop portal, and records with the pinned LGPL FFmpeg bundle.
- Exercise VAAPI hardware encoding where the driver stack exposes it;
  otherwise expect a software fallback path.
- Prove packaging, checksums, and updater metadata under the **private**
  `candidates/linux-alpha/` prefix.

## Not included yet

- No public download on videorc.com and no flip of Linux Alpha update
  pointers.
- Promotion to `releases/linux-alpha/` and named-box packaged smoke remain
  follow-ups after internal acceptance.

Linux Alpha is early software. Treat this AppImage as a test artifact, not a
supported production install.
