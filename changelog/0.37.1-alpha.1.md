## Improvements

- **Windows memory in Health**: Diagnostics shows the engine's memory and its FFmpeg processes on Windows, and Health's Memory check warns at 1 GB and 2 GB there too.
- **The Background row matches the Studio**: the Background row under Camera now looks like Captions and Chat, with no fill and a chevron on the right, in the Retro LCD too. Its small picture shows the Orc for your current pick.
- **The Orc on the Background tiles**: Off and the green and blue screens show the Orc in front of what viewers see behind you.

## Fixes

- **No yellow border on Windows**: the yellow line Windows draws around a captured display no longer shows up in recordings or on streams. A faint outline that only you see marks the captured display instead.
- **Vertical scenes show your screen again**: with 9:16 on, Screen + camera and Side by side showed only the camera and said "No screen". The four default scenes are repaired once, and a 9:16 layout you pick later is kept.
- **No stray scene-change error**: a scene change whose engine connection had already closed no longer shows "Couldn't confirm the scene change".
