This is the next Windows and Linux Alpha after 0.9.143-alpha.1.

## Banners

Import a PNG, JPEG or WebP in Assets → Banners and place it anywhere on the
picture from the Scene page. Transparency is kept, and the inspector tells you
when an image will look soft at the size you gave it. The vertical stream gets
its own position for each banner.

Show and hide banners from the Scene page, the Stream Manager's Banners menu,
`/banner` in the chat composer, ⌘K, a Stream Deck key, the phone remote, or by
asking Buddy. Up to four show at once. A banner can hide itself after a while,
or come back every few minutes for sponsor rotation. Banners are free for
everyone. Some Windows recordings can't add a banner after they start; show
it before you press Record.

## Buddy on stream

Say "Buddy, show up" or "Buddy, come on screen" to put Buddy on the stream, and
"Buddy, hide" or "Buddy, go away" to take it off. While you're live with Buddy
on but not on stream, Buddy's pane in the Stream Manager says so, with a Show
on stream button.

When Videorc AI doesn't answer for a moment, Buddy now says it's retrying
instead of looking stopped.

## Live chat

New messages appear at the bottom and the chat stays with them. Only your own
scroll up stops it, and Buddy marking a message you're talking about no longer
moves the list. While you read back, a chip shows how many messages are new and
takes you back to the newest one.

## Clean cut

Buddy's Clean cut lists your recordings as rows with their poster frame,
length and when they ran, so you can tell them apart. Search appears once you
have more than six.
