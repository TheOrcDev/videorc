## Fixes

- The performance check no longer borrows the Preview to draw full-frame test
  patterns, so the first launch after an update shows your scene instead of
  coloured noise.
- When the Clips replay ring starts before the Preview at launch, the Preview
  still shows your program, including Streaming Buddy, instead of waiting until
  you start recording.

## Under the hood

- Smoke tests stop the whole build when they time out, and CI is faster.
