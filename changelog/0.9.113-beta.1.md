## Connect Kick

In Livestream → Setup, choose **Connect** on the Kick card and sign in on
kick.com. From then on Videorc reads your stream key when you go live, so
there is nothing to copy, and a key you rotate on Kick is picked up on the
next stream. Manual RTMP stays available if you prefer it.

## Title, category, chat and viewers

Broadcast info gets a Kick row: your title and a category search. Kick chat
shows in the Stream Manager and the Comments window with avatars and
badges, and **Send to** can reply on Kick. Orcle reads Kick messages like
any other platform. The stats bar counts Kick viewers, and Activity lists
new Kick follows as a count, since Kick does not say who followed.

Kick delivers chat only through webhooks, so Videorc receives it through
videorc.com. Each message is kept only long enough to show it and deleted
within 24 hours; see the privacy policy for details.
