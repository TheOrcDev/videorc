## The Stream Manager

The Chat window is now the **Stream Manager**: your chat, your audience and the
health of your stream in one window, across every destination you are live on.
Open it from the status bar, or turn on **Open Stream Manager when I go live**
in Settings to have it open by itself.

A row of stats along the top shows how long you have been live, how many people
are watching right now (with the peak and a small chart), your followers and
how many you gained this stream, subs and gifts, tips, how fast chat is moving,
and whether each destination is healthy. Hover a number to see it per platform.
When a platform cannot give a number, the stat says why instead of showing zero.

## Activity

Follows, subs and resubs, gifted subs, cheers, raids, announcements and
destination problems get their own feed, separate from chat. Filter it, see a
summary for the stream, put an event on stream with the highlight card, or
start a thank-you in chat.

## A better chat

Chat shows Twitch emotes, role badges, the message someone replied to, and a
marker on a viewer's first message. Filter by platform, questions or mentions of
you, and search with ⌘F. Each message's ⋯ menu puts it on stream, starts a
reply or copies it. **Send to** picks which platforms get your message; with X
picked, the message keeps to X's 140 characters. Busy chats stay smooth, and
scrolling up pauses the feed until you jump back.

Orcle moves into its own pane next to chat; ⌘J opens it.

## Viewer counts include X

The watching count now adds your X viewers to YouTube and Twitch, and the
per-platform split lists X. The total no longer jumps between platforms when
you stream to Twitch and X together.

## Twitch follow alerts and your sub count

Follow alerts and your Twitch sub total need one more Twitch permission. Choose
**Reconnect Twitch** in Livestream → Setup to grant it; reconnecting later keeps
it.

## Fits any width

The Stream Manager arranges itself for the space it has: chat beside Activity
or Orcle when it is wide, one pane at a time in between, and a one-line summary
above chat in a narrow sidebar.
