This is the next Windows and Linux Alpha after 0.9.147-alpha.1.

## Soundboard

- On Linux, sounds now go into your recording and every stream while a
  microphone is selected. Before, they only reached the output with the
  microphone off. If a session can't carry sounds, Studio says so instead of
  claiming they play on stream.
- On Linux, "Hear sounds yourself" plays the sounds. If a sound can't play on
  your computer, Videorc tells you which one.
- A sound pressed from a Stream Deck or your phone plays right away, even
  while another remote action is still running. A press that waited too long
  is refused instead of playing at the wrong moment.
- Two different sound keys pressed together both play.
- Keys 1 to 9 use the number row, so they press pads on AZERTY and other
  layouts.
- When you import sounds, Videorc names any file it couldn't open and imports
  the rest.
- Stop all shows only while a sound is playing.
