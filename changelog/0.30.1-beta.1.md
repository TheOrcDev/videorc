## Improvements

- **Simpler camera key controls**: the Camera row in Studio, Retro LCD and the Scene inspector now has one compact choice, Off, Green screen or Blue screen, instead of a switch and an Adjust button. Turning it off keeps your saved color and tuning, and detailed tuning is still on the Scene page. (#1426)

## Fixes

- When output stalls for a while, Videorc no longer keeps re-syncing your microphone every few seconds. Capture keeps running and audio picks up cleanly once output catches up. (#1431)
- Background video repair runs at a lower priority and waits while your computer is under heavy load, so it competes less with recording and streaming. (#1429)
