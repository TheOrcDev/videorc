This is the next Windows and Linux Alpha after 0.9.126-alpha.1.

## Weak Windows PCs can go live

Some Windows PCs, especially ones with older Intel graphics, could not go live
while recording. Go Live refused with a long error about a separate encoded
output, and lowering the bitrate did not help. Videorc now records and streams
from one shared encode when the PC cannot run two, and the recording follows
the stream's quality for that session.

- Clicking Go Live while Videorc is still checking your output now waits for
  the check instead of failing.
- Errors are one plain notice, without hardware error codes.
- On PCs with Intel graphics, **Settings → Recording → Fallback video encoder
  (Beta)** can use Intel Quick Sync. It is off by default.
- The performance check has a new 540p rung. A PC that cannot hold 720p streams
  at the largest size that held steady.

## YouTube on busy days

YouTube chat could stop partway through a stream and stay on "reconnecting".
Videorc now asks YouTube for new chat far less often. If YouTube's daily limit
for Videorc is used up, YouTube chat, viewer and subscriber counts pause with
one notice that says when they come back, and they resume on their own. Your
stream keeps going the whole time.

- Stopping a stream never blocks your next recording or stream.
- If YouTube can't create the broadcast when you go live, Videorc offers your
  stream key instead, and your other destinations go live as usual.
- Connecting YouTube during a pause shows one clear message instead of a pile
  of error popups.

## Highlighted comments look right

A comment you highlight on stream now shows the viewer's profile picture and
their Twitch, 7TV and Kick emotes. Animated emotes show as a still image.

## Going live says Streaming

Going live used to show "Recording" and a clock stuck at 0:00. Studio now reads
"Streaming", with a Rec badge when you also record, and the clock ticks. The
Stream Manager shows ON AIR with stream health, and "Open Stream Manager when I
go live" opens it again.

## Activity shows what is on stream

When you put a follow, sub or raid on stream from Activity, its row shows On
stream and its menu offers Remove from stream.

## Audio

- Sources has an audio mixer with one channel strip per source: a level meter
  and Gain fader for the microphone, a mute button that shows its shortcut, a
  Sync slider you can type in, and a Level fader for system audio.
- Studio has a Microphone section with the microphone picker and a level that
  runs whenever Studio is open. The level shows what your recording gets.

## Smaller changes

- Your own messages on X show the Host badge, as they already did on YouTube,
  Twitch and Kick.
- X chat avatars are sharper.

Windows remains an Alpha. See the [Windows Alpha notes](https://www.videorc.com/windows-alpha)
for supported hardware and current limitations.
