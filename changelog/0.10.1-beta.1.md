## LCD level meters

The Studio microphone meter and the Sources meters for Microphone, System
audio and Sounds now draw on an LCD-style screen. You get block segments, a
held peak, the level in digits, a printed scale and a CLIP flag when you go
too loud. It follows your light or dark theme.

## Copy on error messages

Every error message now offers Copy, so you can paste the full details into
a bug report or a message to us.

## Fixes

Cancelling a Buddy pet sheet right after you start it now reports the
cancel, not a missing creation.
