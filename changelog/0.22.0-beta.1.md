## New

- **Viewer pictures in the chat box**: each chat row on stream shows the viewer's picture as a small circle beside their name, or their initials when they have none. (#1281)
- **Hide edges** for the green screen: Left, Top, Right and Bottom fields under Spill removal make those edges of the camera see-through, green or not. (#1291)

## Fixes

- The green screen keys out the screen where it falls into shadow, so dark corners no longer show the wall behind it. (#1291)
