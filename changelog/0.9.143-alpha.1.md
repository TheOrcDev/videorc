## Buddy fix

When you first set Buddy's chat posting to Suggest or Auto, the Stream
Manager asks whether your Buddy may post as you. That prompt showed the stone
golem emblem even when you had picked Golmar the Orc or made your own Buddy.
It now shows the Buddy it names.
