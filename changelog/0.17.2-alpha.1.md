## Fixes

- Switching pages no longer carries the previous page's scroll position.
  Every page opens at its top, so Studio's docked live preview stays visible
  after you scroll down to the canvas on Scene.
- When first-run setup closes, the Clips replay ring now waits for the screen
  preview to produce frames, so the first session starts instead of being
  refused.
- Stopping the preview now leaves its status stopped; a late frame report can
  no longer bring it back.

## Under the hood

- Videorc's menus, dialogs, popovers, sliders, toggles and other controls are
  rebuilt on Base UI. They look and work as before; Shift+D still flips the
  theme.
