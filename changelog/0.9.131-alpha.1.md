This is the next Windows and Linux Alpha after 0.9.129-alpha.1.

## Meet Orcle

The Publish tab is gone. In its place is **Orcle**, Videorc's AI producer, with
its own tab under Studio. Orcle Live is one switch: while you stream, Orcle
reads your chat, collects the questions you haven't answered, flags trouble,
puts the right comment on screen and keeps a report you can read after the
stream. Orcle is part of Videorc Premium.

The Orcle tab is organized into tabs, the same way Settings works, and Orcle
appears as its official eye emblem throughout the app.

## Talk to Orcle

Say "Orcle, highlight the comment from coders X" and that comment goes on your
stream. "Orcle, take it down" clears it.

Say "This one is toxic. Remove it from our chat." and Orcle shows you the
comment first. Nothing is removed until you say "yes" or press Enter, and the
card goes away after 20 seconds if you don't answer. Orcle never acts on its
own.

Removal works on YouTube, Twitch, Kick and X. Twitch and Kick need one new
permission: reconnect them once when Videorc asks. If a platform can't remove a
message, Videorc hides it in Videorc and tells you viewers may still see it.
"Remove from chat" is also in every comment's menu, free for everyone.

See [Talk to Orcle](https://www.videorc.com/orcle#talk-to-orcle) for every
phrase.

## Fixes

- Portrait recordings respect their size limits, and the quality label reads
  by the short edge (1080p for a 1080×1920 recording).
- Scenes keep the sources you picked, including a camera you turned off.
- Unread chat arrivals are kept when the chat buffer rolls over.
- Stream Manager totals cover the whole session.
- "Clip that" marks belong to the recording that heard them.
- The microphone level shows before you start a session.
- Stopping from the phone remote no longer replays an old stop.

Windows remains an Alpha. See the [Windows Alpha notes](https://www.videorc.com/windows-alpha)
for supported hardware and current limitations.
