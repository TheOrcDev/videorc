## Buddy talks on its own

Buddy now greets people in your chat without you writing a line first. Every
kind has a built-in greeting: first-time chatters get "Hi {name}, welcome to
the stream!", returning chatters get "Welcome back, {name}!", and follows,
subs, raids and the rest have their own short line. Edit any of them, or
switch one off, in Buddy → Chat → Greetings.

With Videorc Premium and Cloud AI on, Buddy writes those greetings in its
own voice. Your line is the example and the fallback if the AI can't write
one. The Stream Manager chip says **Greetings on · AI** when that's allowed.

Buddy can also answer a question nobody has answered after a minute, when
your notes have the answer. Turn Answers on in Buddy → Chat if it was off.

Nothing goes to your chat unless you choose: posting stays Off until you
set it to Suggest or Auto in the Stream Manager.

## Live backgrounds on stream

Live backgrounds now move on the live stream, not only in Studio and the
recording. A 4K 60 fps session, or a stream above 1080p 60, still shows a
still frame and says so, as before.
