## Emotes in chat

Kick emotes used to appear as text codes in the Stream Manager. They now show
as images. 7TV emotes show as images too, and new emotes added to a channel
appear within about half a minute.

## YouTube chat sends again

Messages sent to YouTube chat from Videorc failed every time. They now arrive.

## A live stream survives a busy computer

Starting a heavy app while live could stall Videorc for a few seconds, and
the stream ended. A stream or recording now waits out a stall of up to 30
seconds and carries on. If something does fail, Videorc tells you while you
are live.

## YouTube live thumbnail

Broadcast info has a thumbnail for your YouTube broadcast. A thumbnail that
cannot be uploaded never stops you going live.

## Smaller changes

- X chat authors show their organization badge beside their name.
