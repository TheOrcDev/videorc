## The real YouTube icon

Everywhere Videorc shows YouTube, you now see YouTube's official icon, in its
own colours: the destination list, Upcoming streams, the Stream Manager, the
highlight card on your stream and the phone remote. It is never shrunk below a
readable size.

## Cleaner chat and activity rows

Stream Manager chat and activity rows put the platform logo on the far right,
so names line up next to the avatar. Avatars are bigger, and every platform
logo is drawn at the same size. Activity no longer squeezes a tiny platform
badge onto the avatar.

The highlight card shows the platform logo on the right of the name row, and
the phone remote shows each viewer's initials with the platform logo on the
right.
