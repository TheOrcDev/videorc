## Features

- Microphone **Noise reduction** (RNNoise) cleans session audio for the live
  stream, recording, captions and Buddy. System audio is untouched. The default
  is Off; the switch remembers your choice. (#1036)

## Fixes

- The Quick Sync probe maps its video, so Intel PCs can pass it. (#1051)
- Captioned copies hand FFmpeg plain paths, and a failed burn says why. (#1125)
- Locale-aware comments totals and two backend test races. (#1173)

## Under the hood

- Smoke and CI gates for silent microphones, 4K60 refusal, camera-extension
  fake paths, and macOS-only CARGO_INCREMENTAL. (#1170, #1172, #1169, #1161,
  #1162)
