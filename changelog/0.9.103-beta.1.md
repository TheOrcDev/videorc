## Schedule X broadcasts

In Livestream → Upcoming, choose **Schedule on X** to plan a broadcast on your X
account: a title, description, thumbnail, start time, planned end, and whether
the replay stays available. X shows the planned length to your followers; you
can stop whenever you like. Edit, duplicate or cancel a broadcast from Upcoming,
and copy its link to share it early.

When it is time, choose **Go Live…** on the broadcast, pick your X destination,
and start from Studio as usual. A scheduled broadcast never starts on its own.
Scheduling needs X Live authorized for your account, the same as going live on X
from Videorc.

## YouTube and X, side by side

Upcoming now gives each platform its own section with its own **Schedule**
button, so a YouTube event and an X broadcast never blur together. Each saved
stream shows its picture, its state (Draft, Scheduled, Live, Ended), where and
when it airs in its own time zone, and one main action. Everything else is in
the ⋯ menu.

Public builds still cannot connect a YouTube account while Google approval is
pending; the YouTube section says so, and drafts stay available.

## The Videorc icon in About

Settings → About & updates opens with the Videorc icon, your version and the
track you are on.
