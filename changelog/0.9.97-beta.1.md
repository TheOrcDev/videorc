## Chat, not Comments

The detached window where you read your viewers has always been chat, so that is
what it is called now. The window, its shortcut, the command palette, the Go
Live dialog and the Library menu all say Chat.

- **It looks like the rest of Videorc.** The Chat and Captions windows now use
  the same glass material as the main window instead of flat black, and the
  header lines up with the window buttons.
- **Put the highlight where you want it.** A new Highlight position menu in the
  Chat header lets you choose top left, top right, bottom left or bottom right.
  It used to always land top centre, which could run under your camera. Your
  pick is remembered and is used even when the Chat window is closed, for
  example when you highlight from a shortcut or a deck. Change it while a
  message is on screen and the card moves there.
- **Easier to read.** The viewer's name now sits with their message instead of
  floating centred above it.
- Captions keep their own position setting and only move out of the way when a
  highlight would actually overlap them.

## Check what your computer can record

Videorc used to start every new install at 1440p30 without knowing whether the
computer could keep up. On a slower machine that meant choppy or lost
recordings.

- **Measured once, by itself.** Videorc makes a few short test recordings, from
  4K30 down to 720p30, and finds the highest output your computer holds
  comfortably. The Recording tab shows the result, marks presets as Verified or
  Too heavy, and one click applies the recommendation. Check again whenever you
  like, for example after changing hardware.
- It uses a generated test picture, so it needs no camera, screen or microphone
  permission, and the test recordings never appear in your Library.
- **A safer starting point.** New installs now begin at 1080p30 until the
  computer has been measured. If you already chose an output, Videorc never
  changes it for you. You only get a warning if it measures too heavy.
- **Stop waits for your file.** If your computer fell behind while recording,
  Stop now gives the recording time to finish writing instead of ending it
  early.

## Good to know

- The check takes a few seconds per step. Starting a recording or a stream
  always wins: the check steps aside immediately.
- The recommendation is for recording. Streaming also depends on your upload
  speed, which the check does not measure.
