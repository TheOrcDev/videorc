## Video beside Audio

The Sources page is rebuilt. Screen and Camera sit on the left, Microphone and
System audio on the right, so pickers, meters and their values sit close
together instead of stretching across the whole window.

## One shape for every source

Every source now has the same layout: its name, at most one status, its one
quick control and a More area for the settings you rarely touch. A device that
disappears says "Not found", and a muted microphone says "Muted". Screen shows
its size and Camera shows its format and frame rate.

The microphone's Sync offset now lives under More. While it is off its default,
a small tag next to the microphone tells you, so a hidden setting never
surprises you. System audio shows its meter and level only while it is on.

## A clean docked preview

When the preview is docked in Studio, it no longer draws a thin grey rim around
the video.
