## New

- **Every scene shows its own screen**: a scene is an app, a window, a display or no screen, and it keeps its own 16:9 and 9:16 layouts. A new profile starts with four scenes: Screen + camera, Screen only, Camera only and Side by side. Your existing scenes move over by themselves.
- **Studio is the home of scenes**: the Scenes section has a card for each scene with a picture of its screen and its position key. Add scene… adds several apps or displays at once without changing what is on stream. Below the cards, Change screen…, Hide screen, Layout and Framing edit the scene that is on stream.
- **Position keys**: ⌃⌥1 to ⌃⌥9 switch to the scene at that position from any app. Reordering scenes moves the keys with them.
- **Screen layout on a scene without a screen**: picking a layout that draws a screen asks which screen to show. Cancel keeps the layout you had.

## Improvements

- **Camera only keeps your screen ready**: switching back to an app scene is instant, with no cold start.
- **Sources shows the screen read-only**: it names the scene that shows it and links to Open Scenes. The Scene page lists scenes as rows.
- **Remotes switch scenes by name**: the phone remote, Stream Deck, MCP and the ⌘K Pages list switch scenes directly.
