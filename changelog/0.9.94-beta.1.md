## Two orientations from one Go Live

- **Horizontal and vertical at the same time.** Keep streaming your landscape
  scene to YouTube, Twitch and X, and add vertical destinations to the same
  session. Videorc composes the vertical picture from your saved vertical
  scene - camera on top, split, camera only, whichever you set up in the
  vertical Studio mode - using the same sources, so nothing is cropped or
  letterboxed.
- **Vertical stream monitor.** While a vertical destination is armed, the
  Studio shows the vertical scene beside the mixer with its Armed or Live
  state, so you always know what the portrait audience sees.
- **Two encodes, not six.** Every landscape destination shares one encode and
  every vertical destination shares the other, so the cost of going wide is
  your upload bandwidth, not your CPU. The Go Live confirmation shows the
  estimated Mbps across all legs before you start.

## TikTok and Instagram

- **TikTok** and **Instagram** cards join the destination list. Both use a
  stream key from the platform: TikTok LIVE Center for accounts with LIVE
  access, Instagram Live Producer for professional accounts. Keys change
  every broadcast, and the card links you to the right place each time.
- **Instagram publishes on its side.** Start streaming in Videorc first, then
  press "Go live" in Live Producer once it shows your picture. The Go Live
  confirmation reminds you of this step.
- **YouTube Vertical** is a second broadcast on your existing YouTube account
  that advertises the portrait profile, so YouTube treats it as a vertical
  live.
- Neither TikTok nor Instagram offers a public chat feed, so the Comments
  window says so instead of pretending: watch their chat in their app while
  you stream.

## Good to know

- Live captions burned into the stream and vertical streaming cannot run
  together yet. Set captions to Off or Recording while a vertical destination
  is armed.
- Edit the vertical scene in the vertical Studio mode; going live from the
  horizontal mode uses the last vertical scene you saved.
