## Multistreaming is now free

- **Every plan can stream to up to five destinations at once.** Enable
  YouTube, Twitch, X, and custom RTMP destinations side by side and go live
  to all of them from one session - no Premium account needed. Videorc
  sends each stream straight from your computer, so the only cost is your
  own upload bandwidth (roughly 6 Mbps per destination).
- **One encode, one shared quality.** All destinations share a single encode,
  so a multistream session runs at the multistream-safe 1080p30 preset. That
  is the same quality the free plan already streamed at, so nothing is lost
  by adding destinations.
- **A plain limit, not an upsell.** If you try to enable a sixth destination,
  the Live tab simply says "You can stream to up to 5 destinations at once."
  There is no upgrade prompt, because there is nothing to upgrade to.
- **What Premium still buys.** Premium continues to fund the features with a
  real server bill or a hardware ceiling: cloud AI publishing (transcripts,
  titles, chapters, publish packs), live captions, the live co-host, and
  higher single-destination streaming quality (1080p60 and 4K30). See
  [videorc.com/premium](https://www.videorc.com/premium).
