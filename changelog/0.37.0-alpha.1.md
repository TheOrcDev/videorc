## New

- **Native Linux cameras**: Videorc reads each camera's own formats and asks for the best native mode for your canvas, instead of scaling a smaller picture up on the CPU, so a camera at idle uses far less CPU. Plugging a camera in or out is picked up while Videorc runs.
- **A smooth Linux Preview**: the Preview now shows every camera frame instead of about eight a second, and heals itself if frames stop. Health reports what the Preview really gets.
- **Hardware encoding by default on Linux**: VAAPI is used by default where the GPU supports it, and a GPU known to hang is skipped reliably, even when its device number changes between boots.
- **Linux setup checks**: Videorc checks for a keyring (Secret Service), access to your camera and GPU devices, and a screen-sharing portal, and tells you what is missing. Signing in without a keyring now shows an error instead of failing silently.
- **1440p and 4K30 recording on Windows**: Record at 1440p and 4K30, landscape or portrait, on the GPU path instead of the slower CPU path.
- **A new Background control**: the Studio's Camera row, the Scene page and ⌘K have one Background control for the green screen key. Blur and pictures come to Windows and Linux in a later update.

## Improvements

- **A quiet launch**: Videorc no longer greets you with a stack of toasts. While nothing is recording or live, what it notices goes to the bell; pop-ups are kept for a recording or stream that fails and for the answer to something you pressed.
- **Calmer alerts**: an alert is never tinted; its tone colours only the icon.
- **A silent microphone is easier to fix**: Open Audio on the silent-mic card goes straight to the microphone, and the card names the likely cause.
- **Truer Linux stream bitrate**: each stream reports its bitrate from what FFmpeg really encoded.

## Fixes

- **Hide screen never leaks**: on Windows and Linux too, a frame taken while the screen was hidden never goes out when you show it again.
- **Linux microphone holds**: the mic keeps going when encoding falls behind real time, and Stop hands over all the audio it owes.
- **Windows Record rides out encoder hiccups**: a short Quick Sync pause no longer drops the recording's frames.
- **Windows Preview placement**: the Preview keeps its place until its picture is live.
- **Stop during a reconnect**: Stop lets go of a stream that is only reconnecting, and a whole recording is never treated as a recovery file.
- **Scene changes stick**: a layout change is never put back by the Clips buffer starting just after it.
- **Clips wait for a quiet camera**: a camera that sends no frames makes Clips back off and wait, instead of retrying forever.
- **No false problem notices**: an idle scene reload that a new session replaced no longer shows "Videorc hit a problem".
- **Security**: a bundled dependency was updated for a published security advisory.
