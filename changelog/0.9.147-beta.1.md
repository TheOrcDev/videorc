## Soundboard

Studio has a Soundboard under Scenes, with twelve sounds streamers use most:
Airhorn, Applause, Laugh, Rimshot, Sad trombone, Drumroll, Tada, Buzzer, Ding,
Crickets, Boom and Woah. Click a pad, or press its key from 1 to 9 while
Studio is in front. While you record or stream, the sound goes into the
recording and every stream at the moment you pressed it. Between sessions
only you hear it, so you can try sounds out.

Add your own sounds in Assets → Sounds: WAV, MP3, M4A, OGG or FLAC, up to 30
seconds each. Each sound can play once, play and stop, loop, or play while
you hold its pad. Videorc measures every sound when you add it and brings it
to the same loudness. Sources → Sounds sets the soundboard's level and
whether you hear the sounds yourself (use headphones, or your microphone may
pick them up again). The Stream Manager has a Sounds button, and `/sound
airhorn` plays a sound from the chat box.

## Clips

Videorc keeps the last 30 seconds of what you see whenever it's open. Press
Clip in the Stream Manager, type `/clip`, or say "clip that" while Buddy or
captions are listening, and those seconds are saved to your Library as a
clip. Turn it off on the Output page.

## Fixes

- Choosing None for Screen stays None when you switch scenes or refresh your
  devices.
- The Library total updates as soon as a recording finishes.
- Studio → Session → Output shows the quality the session actually records
  while you're live.
