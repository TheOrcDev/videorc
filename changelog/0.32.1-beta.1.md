## Fixes

- Record and Stop pressed from a phone or deck were never noted as remote presses, so their latency was labeled as an in-app call. They are now read correctly, which makes the Record and Stop latency numbers trustworthy. (#1535)

## Under the hood

- Build and test hardening: the macOS test compile has more time on a loaded runner, and the layout and preview smokes handle the docked Preview correctly. (#1434, #1261)
