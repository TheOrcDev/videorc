## Updates

- **Restart to update**, in the sidebar and in Settings → About, works again
  while Clips is on. Before, the button did nothing, because Clips keeps a
  session running in the background and Videorc treated it like a recording.
- A real recording or stream still holds the update until it ends.

If you're on 0.9.149 or earlier, the button there still does nothing. Quit
Videorc, wait a few seconds, then open it again: the update installs while
it's closed.
