## GIFs in chat

When a Twitch subscriber sends a GIF from Twitch's GIF Keyboard, the Stream
Manager now shows the GIF itself instead of its title in brackets.

## Members stand out

Rows from YouTube members and Twitch, Kick and X subscribers now have a quiet
tint in the Stream Manager chat, next to the Member chip, so you can spot your
supporters at a glance.
