## Livestream setup in plain words

Each destination card now asks how you want to connect: **Sign in** or
**Stream key**. A signed-in account is one row with your avatar, name and one
status. When something needs you, the card says "Needs reconnect" and offers
Reconnect.

The right column is now **Ready to go live**. It names each destination that
is not ready and opens its card when you click it. Quality, upload needed and
disk use are stated once, and the technical details are folded away until you
want them.

## A microphone preview that works

The live waveform in Sources and the idle bars in the Studio mixer were flat
for every Mac microphone, and Sources blamed permission. Both show your real
level again, and the message names the actual cause when a preview cannot
start. Plugging a microphone in or out retries the preview.

## Screen and Camera in one click

Studio Inputs has separate **Screen** and **Camera** rows. Each row is the
list itself: Screen shows your screens and windows under their own headings,
and Camera starts with Off. Choosing Off for the camera now sticks.

## Smaller fixes

- The sidebar update chip reads "Restart to update" and no longer truncates.
- Settings section headings no longer carry icons.
- If the Videorc window stops drawing, it reloads by itself. If that keeps
  happening, Videorc asks whether to reload or quit.
