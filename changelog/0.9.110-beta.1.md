## Smaller handles on the live Scene canvas

The frame and the eight resize handles that 0.9.109 draws on the live
picture were sized for a bigger canvas than the one on screen, so they came
out about twice as large as intended. They are now small, sharp squares with
a hairline frame, and they stay that size whether the window is narrow or
wide.
