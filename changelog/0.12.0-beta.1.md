## 1-minute clips

Clips used to save one length for every press. Now there are two, and you
choose when you press: 30 seconds, which is unchanged and still the default,
or 1 minute.

- The Stream Manager status bar shows **Clip 30 s** and **1 min** side by
  side.
- Settings → Shortcuts has a second shortcut for the 1-minute clip. It is
  unbound until you set it. Your existing clip shortcut keeps saving 30
  seconds.
- Type `/clip` or `/clip 30` for 30 seconds and `/clip 60` for 1 minute in the
  Stream Manager composer. ⌘K has both too.
- By voice, "clip that" saves 30 seconds, and "clip the last minute" or "long
  clip" saves 1 minute.
- The Output → Clips length row is gone, since each press now picks its own
  length. If you had set a different length there, it goes back to 30
  seconds.

A 1-minute clip keeps about twice as much on disk while Clips is on, roughly
68 MB at 8 Mbps and 332 MB at 40 Mbps. CPU and GPU use do not change.

## Fixes

- Clips comes back after its buffer stops, and that is no longer reported as a
  failed recording.
- Idle soundboard pad presses no longer end up in your clips.
- With separate recordings, the Screen file survives a stale recovery latch,
  and Clips stops looping.
- A scene that shows a source you turned off now waits with a plain message
  instead of an error.
- "Recording stopped unexpectedly" and "Livestream stopped unexpectedly" now
  have a Copy button, so you can paste the error to support.
