## A Sources audio mixer

Sources now has an audio mixer with one channel strip per source, so it reads
like a small console.

- **Microphone:** its state, the same level meter as Studio, a Gain fader from
  −24 to +24 dB (double-click returns it to 0 dB), and a mute button that shows
  its keyboard shortcut.
- **Sync:** a slider with a millisecond field you can type in or drag, plus
  Reset. While you record or stream it tells you the change applies from the
  next session.
- **System audio:** its state, a meter while you record or stream, a Level
  fader from −24 to +12 dB, and the On switch.

The calibration tools fold under Calibrate until you need them.

## Smaller changes

- A recording plus stream that needed a second encoder now shares one, instead
  of refusing to start.
