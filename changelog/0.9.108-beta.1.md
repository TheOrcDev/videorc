The Stream Manager showed everything at once. Now it shows what matters
first: more room for chat, and less to read while you are live.

## One thin stats bar

The row of stat tiles above chat was tall and mostly empty. It is now one
thin bar. How long you have been live, how many people are watching and
your stream health sit together at the left, at every window size,
including the narrow sidebar size where stream health used to be missing.

Followers, subs, tips and chat pace follow as plain numbers, and drop off
the end when the window is narrow. Point at any stat for the details: the
split per platform, your peak, frame rate, dropped frames and the state of
each destination.

Stream health stays quiet while everything is fine and shows your bitrate.
When a destination fails, frames drop or the bitrate sags, it says so right
there instead.

## Make the bar yours

Drag a stat to move it. Right-click the bar to hide the stats you don't
watch, move one left or right, or reset it. The clock and the viewer count
always stay up front.

## Less to read

- A chat message shows its time when you point at it, not on every row.
- Replies read "↳ @name", a message that mentions you shows "@you", and
  the paused chip just says how many messages are new.
- The message box no longer repeats where a message goes. It speaks up only
  when a destination can't receive it, and a message that sends leaves
  nothing behind.
- Activity rows read at a glance, like "Resub · 14 months" or "Raid · 142
  viewers". The highlight card your viewers see keeps the full sentence.
- The status bar shows each platform as its icon and a green dot while chat
  works, and adds words only when something is wrong.

Chat also stays on the newest message after you resize the window or a
message grows as its emotes load.
