## New

- **Background blur and pictures**: each camera now has its own Background setting: Off, a Slight or Strong blur, a picture from your Images, or the green screen key. The effect reaches the recording, every stream, Clips and the Preview. While the effect gets ready, or if it can't keep up, viewers see a fully blurred picture or your picture alone, never your real background.
- **One Background control everywhere**: the Studio's Camera row has a Background button with a live "What viewers see" picture and tiles for Blur, Images and the green screen. The Scene page's Background section has the same tiles plus Blur strength, ⌘K has Blur background and Turn off background effect, and the Retro LCD shows your pick.
- **macOS camera effects are named**: when macOS Video Effects, such as its own background blur, change your camera before Videorc sees it, Videorc says so, because viewers get those effects too.

## Improvements

- **A quiet launch**: Videorc no longer greets you with a stack of toasts. While nothing is recording or live, what it notices goes to the bell; pop-ups are kept for a recording or stream that fails and for the answer to something you pressed.
- **Calmer alerts**: an alert is never tinted; its tone colours only the icon.
- **A silent microphone is easier to fix**: Open Audio on the silent-mic card goes straight to the microphone, and the card names the likely cause.

## Fixes

- **Hide screen never leaks**: a frame taken while the screen was hidden never goes out when you show it again, and a frame from the very moment of Show is held back too.
- **Scene changes stick**: a layout change is never put back by the Clips buffer starting just after it.
- **Clips wait for a quiet camera**: a camera that sends no frames makes Clips back off and wait, instead of retrying forever.
- **No false problem notices**: an idle scene reload that a new session replaced no longer shows "Videorc hit a problem".
- **Background tile names wrap** under their picture instead of being cut off.
- **Security**: a bundled dependency was updated for a published security advisory.
