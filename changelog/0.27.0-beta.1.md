## New

- **Avatar and Sidekick, two products**: the character on your stream is now the **Avatar**, with its own page under Setup (⌘6) for its look, name and personality, reactions, motion and greetings. **Sidekick** is the helper that works your chat in the Stream Manager and stays under Livestream (⌘9). Sidekick writes, the avatar says it. (#1372)
- **A calm Preview start**: the blue-to-green striped screen that filled the Preview while Videorc started is gone. The Studio stage now shows which startup step is running. (#1369)
- **Green screen on the Camera row**: the Studio's Camera row has a Green screen switch (Blue screen while a blue key is on) and an **Adjust** button. ⌘K finds it by "green screen", "chroma", "remove background" and more, and the Green screen section is now first in the camera's inspector on the Scene page. (#1368)

## Fixes

- **Stream keys stay out of the database**: starting a recording or livestream no longer saves your stream keys in plain text in Videorc's settings database, and copies saved there by earlier versions are deleted. Keys stay in your system's secret store. (#1364)
