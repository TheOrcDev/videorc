## Kick

Going live on Kick failed straight away in 0.9.113 and 0.9.114, with an
"Input/output error" and nothing reaching your channel. Kick hands out its
ingest address without the part its servers expect, and Videorc used it as
it was. Videorc now builds the full address from what Kick returns, so a
connected Kick account goes live again. Nothing needs to be reconnected:
Videorc reads your Kick stream key each time you go live.

## Stream keys stay private

When a stream could not connect, the error Videorc logged included the full
destination address, and for RTMP that address contains your stream key.
Stream keys and any password in a server address are now masked in the log,
in the in-app log and in support bundles. If you shared a support bundle
after a failed stream, consider resetting that platform's stream key.

## Connected

After you sign in to YouTube, Twitch, Kick or X, the browser tab now shows a
Videorc page that says you're connected and names the platform, instead of a
bare line of text. If sign-in failed, it says why and what to try next.
