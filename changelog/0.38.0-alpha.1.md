## New

- **X live categories**: Stream details lists X's categories in X's order. Go Live sends your pick, and a scheduled X show gets it once it's live.
- **Edit X while live**: while an X broadcast is on air, a Live on X section above Stream details changes its title and category on X at once.

## Improvements

- **Overlays drawn by the engine**: the caption bar, the chat box, the highlight and milestone cards and the Sidekick bubble are drawn by the engine, so nothing on stream depends on the app window.
- **Microphone meters on Windows and Linux**: the idle meters, the monitor and the join chime come from the engine.
- **Quieter while live**: during a recording or livestream only an urgent problem pops up. Everything else waits behind the bell.

## Fixes

- **Background picture adjustments apply**: Blur, Dim, Saturation and Vignette from Adjust style now reach the stream and the recording.
- **Numpad shortcuts**: numpad keys read as their keycaps (Num 4, Num +), and global shortcuts refuse AltGr chords.
- **Scheduled times**: scheduled stream times are right in time zones that changed in the latest time zone data.
