## YouTube uses less of its daily limit

YouTube gives Videorc a shared daily limit for chat, viewer counts and going
live. Videorc now checks YouTube viewer counts every 2 minutes and subscriber
counts every 5 minutes, so more of that limit is left for chat during long
streams.

Every request Videorc makes to YouTube now counts toward its daily budget,
including scheduled streams, sign-in checks, retries and the steps that end a
broadcast. When the limit is close, Videorc's pause notice is accurate instead
of arriving late.

## Viewer counts on other platforms

The slower YouTube schedule used to hold back viewer counts from Twitch, Kick
and X too. Each platform now updates on its own, and the others still refresh
every minute.
