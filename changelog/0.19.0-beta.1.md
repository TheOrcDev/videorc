## New

- Videorc Camera: your program as a camera in Meet and Zoom. (#1091, #1092)
- Move and resize the scene while recording or live. (#1059)
- Chat on stream: the last five messages, off by default. (#1095, #1114)
- Freeform: the screen's box is its picture, and Freeform and other
  rearrangements never wait for a source that's already on. (#1102, #1100)
- The canvas drags on the fallback preview. (#1098)
- The Stream Manager can stay on top and off your stream. (#1058)
- Edit scene on the Scene page and in ⌘K. (#1056)
- Sources bigger than the canvas are cut off, never squashed, with a screen
  Zoom up to 400%. (#1094)
- X chat names the missing Videorc sign-in, and attaches when you sign in. (#1060)
- A camera that sends no video says so. (#1097)

## Fixes

- Camera look records on the shipped LGPL FFmpeg. (#1047, #1050)
- The Stream Manager status bar fits at 640 and 800 px while live, and chat
  rows have no stripe down the left. (#1112, #1110)
- Buddy's typed answer survives a Clips ring start. (#1107)
- A client camera stop is refused while a separate take records the camera. (#1116)
- The camera's Size and Shape fit on one line. (#1045)
- The Preview watchdog stands down while a docked Preview is hidden on
  purpose, and a proof-surface call settles when the surface closes. (#1040, #1115)
- Continuity Camera labels no longer repeat iPhone. (#1043)
