## Phone remote

Keep your chat in your hand while you stream. A phone on the same Wi-Fi as your
computer becomes a live chat monitor and a control deck.

- **Scan and go.** Turn on Phone remote in Settings, scan the QR code, and the
  remote opens in your phone's browser. There is nothing to install and no
  account to sign in to on the phone.
- **Read every message.** Chat from all your connected platforms appears on the
  phone while you are live.
- **Put a message on stream with a tap.** Tap a message to show it on your
  video, the same highlight you get from the Chat window, in the corner you
  picked there. Tap it again to take it off.
- **A deck in your pocket.** Switch scenes, show a takeover, mute your
  microphone, and start or stop. Stop is press and hold, so a stray tap never
  ends your stream.

## Private by design

- The remote works over your own Wi-Fi, not the internet, and it is off until
  you turn it on.
- The QR code works once and expires after five minutes. Every phone gets its
  own key, and everything the phone sends is signed, so another device on your
  Wi-Fi cannot take control or replay your taps.
- Your phones are listed in Settings. Rename one, or remove it and it stops
  working immediately. Your Stream Deck and other phones stay paired.
- Phones receive message text and names only, and only while one is connected.

## Updates that get through

Some networks block one of the big storage providers for hours at a time. In
Spain this happens on football match evenings, and it used to stop Videorc from
downloading or updating until the block ended.

- Videorc releases now live on two independent servers. If the first one cannot
  be reached, the app quietly tries the second.
- The download page has a "Try the mirror" link under the download button for
  the same situation.
- Nothing changes in how you update. It just works on more networks.

## Good to know

- Your phone and computer need to be on the same Wi-Fi. The first time you turn
  Phone remote on, macOS may ask whether Videorc can accept incoming
  connections. Choose Allow.
- Turning Phone remote on also turns on Remote control.
- You cannot send chat messages from the phone yet.
- We also tightened what paired control devices such as a Stream Deck can see
  of the app. Nothing changes in how you use them.
