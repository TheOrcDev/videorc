This is the next Windows and Linux Alpha after 0.9.146-alpha.1.

## Soundboard

Studio has a Soundboard under Scenes, with twelve sounds streamers use most:
Airhorn, Applause, Laugh, Rimshot, Sad trombone, Drumroll, Tada, Buzzer, Ding,
Crickets, Boom and Woah. Click a pad, or press its key from 1 to 9 while
Studio is in front. While you record or stream, the sound goes into the
recording and every stream at the moment you pressed it.

Add your own sounds in Assets → Sounds: WAV, MP3, M4A, OGG or FLAC, up to 30
seconds each. Videorc brings every sound to the same loudness. Sources →
Sounds sets the soundboard's level and whether you hear the sounds yourself.

## Clips

Videorc keeps the last 30 seconds of what you see whenever it's open. Press
Clip in the Stream Manager, type `/clip`, or say "clip that" while Buddy or
captions are listening, and those seconds are saved to your Library as a
clip. Turn it off on the Output page. On a computer that encodes video in
software it starts off, and the Output page says why.

## Fixes

- Output → Local recording keeps Separate source recordings off on Windows
  and Linux and says why, so Record no longer fails with it on. It works on
  Mac only for now.
- On Linux, takeovers and banners you import show in Assets and in the
  preview again.
- Settings → About says Alpha on Linux.
- Choosing None for Screen stays None when you switch scenes or refresh your
  devices.
- The Library total updates as soon as a recording finishes.

## Known issue

On Linux you don't hear soundboard sounds yourself yet. They still play in
your recordings and streams.
