## Connect YouTube

Google approved Videorc for YouTube sign-in. In Livestream → Setup, choose
**Connect** on the YouTube card and sign in with Google. Videorc creates a
fresh broadcast when you go live, so there is nothing to copy from YouTube
Studio. Manual RTMP stays available if you prefer a key you already made.

YouTube chat shows in the Stream Manager and Comments, and **Send to** can
reply on YouTube. Upcoming can schedule a YouTube livestream.

## YouTube Vertical

OAuth creates a new broadcast every session. A YouTube Vertical manual key
tied to Studio's Dual stream setting does not land on that broadcast. If you
stream YouTube Vertical beside horizontal YouTube, keep the horizontal card
on Manual RTMP for this cut.
