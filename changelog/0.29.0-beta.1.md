## New

- **videorc.com/live shows your stream**: while you're signed in and live, Videorc keeps videorc.com/live up to date with what your stream is doing, so you can check on it from any browser. It runs in the background and never touches your recording or stream. (#1397, #1398, #1403)
- **Save my streams on videorc.com**: After the stream has a new switch that keeps your stream history on videorc.com, and a Your streams row that opens videorc.com/live. (#1401, #1404)

## Fixes

- Disconnecting YouTube also clears the YouTube statistics saved on videorc.com, and tries again on the next launch if that first attempt fails. (#1402)
