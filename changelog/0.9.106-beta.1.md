Videorc can now take one session to landscape and portrait destinations
together, keep chat in the room with Orcle, and let you change the picture
without tearing down the take. This build also fixes the microphone dropouts
that started in 0.9.103.

## Go Live

One Go Live can send your landscape scene to YouTube, Twitch and X while your
saved vertical scene streams to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Vertical. The
portrait picture is a real 9:16 composition of your sources, never a crop of
the landscape frame. Landscape destinations share one encode and vertical
destinations share the other, so going wide costs upload bandwidth, not a
pile of extra encodes.

In Livestream → Upcoming, schedule an X broadcast with a title, description,
thumbnail, start time, planned end and whether the replay stays available,
then choose **Go Live** when you are ready. Nothing starts on its own.
YouTube events sit in their own Upcoming section. Public builds still cannot
connect a YouTube account while Google approval is pending.

## Chat and Orcle

Orcle is your live chat co-host. Write custom rules in plain language (no
spoilers, English only), see richer flags when a message is a problem, and
look for a Suggested badge on comments worth putting on stream. You still
decide. Orcle never posts without you.

Comments from a native X broadcast now land in the same studio feed as
YouTube and Twitch, with names and avatars. If you already authorized X Live,
they just start arriving.

## Studio and layouts

Change your camera, microphone, display or window while you record or stream.
The file keeps writing and your destinations stay connected. Videorc shows
the change as pending until the new source is really in use, and tells you
if a device could not switch so you can retry.

Save a complete scene (sources, layout, framing, background) and return to
it, including while recording. Each layout remembers its own framing. Search
the source picker, set your own layout shortcuts, and arrange the canvas by
hand: drag, resize, type exact sizes, or choose Freeform and place sources
anywhere.

## Stream Manager and remote

The Chat window is now the **Stream Manager**: live viewers (including X),
followers, subs, tips, an activity feed and stream health next to your chat.
Open it from the status bar, or turn on **Open Stream Manager when I go live**.

A phone on the same Wi-Fi becomes a chat monitor and a control deck. Scan a
QR code in Settings. There is nothing to install. Tap a message to put it on
stream, switch scenes, mute, or press and hold to stop.

## Reliability

Videorc can measure your computer with a few short test recordings and
recommend an output it can actually hold. Run the check from the Recording
tab whenever you like. Every window now sits on real macOS glass.

Since 0.9.103, a recording or stream could lose short pieces of microphone
audio: tiny gaps that sounded like clicks or a crackle, worst on USB
microphones such as the Shure MV7+. This build gives microphone audio a full
second of headroom and keeps listening even while the file writer is briefly
held up. If audio is ever lost, the session log says how much and why.
