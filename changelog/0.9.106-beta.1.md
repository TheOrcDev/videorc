Since 0.9.103, a recording or stream could lose short pieces of microphone
audio: tiny gaps that sounded like clicks or a crackle, several times a
second on a bad take. Microphones that hand audio to the Mac in small pieces,
such as the Shure MV7+ over USB, were hit hardest, and the built-in microphone
could be affected under load. The microphone itself was never at fault.

This build gives microphone audio a full second of headroom on its way into
the recording, keeps listening to the microphone even while the file writer is
briefly held up, and no longer starves the encoder's audio queue. Every
microphone gets the same headroom no matter how it delivers audio.

If Videorc ever does lose microphone audio, the session log now records how
much was lost and why, so the cause can be found instead of guessed.
