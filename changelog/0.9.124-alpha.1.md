This is the next Windows and Linux Alpha after 0.9.122-alpha.1. It adds
YouTube sign-in, plus the live-caption and toast changes that already shipped
on macOS 0.9.123.

## Connect YouTube

Google approved Videorc for YouTube sign-in. In Livestream → Setup, choose
**Connect** on the YouTube card and sign in with Google. Videorc creates a
fresh broadcast when you go live, so there is nothing to copy from YouTube
Studio. Manual RTMP stays available if you prefer a key you already made.

YouTube chat shows in the Stream Manager and Comments, and **Send to** can
reply on YouTube. Upcoming can schedule a YouTube livestream.

OAuth creates a new broadcast every session. A YouTube Vertical manual key
tied to Studio's Dual stream setting does not land on that broadcast. If you
stream YouTube Vertical beside horizontal YouTube, keep the horizontal card
on Manual RTMP for this cut.

## Live captions on vertical streams

When you streamed to a vertical destination (YouTube Shorts, TikTok,
Instagram) at the same time as a horizontal one, live captions could show in
the app and never reach the video. Turning captions on after you went live
had the same gap.

Captions now burn on both streams. The vertical captions are sized for a
phone screen and sit clear of the TikTok, Shorts and Reels button bands. The
recording also carries the captions while a vertical destination is on.

## Toasts stay clear

A warning or error toast used to wash the whole panel with amber or red.
Every toast is the same glass as the rest of the app. Success, warning, error
and info only colour the 16 px icon.

Windows remains an Alpha. See the [Windows Alpha notes](https://www.videorc.com/windows-alpha)
for supported hardware and current limitations.
