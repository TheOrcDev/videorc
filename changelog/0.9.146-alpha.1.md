This is the next Windows and Linux Alpha after 0.9.144-alpha.1.

## Discord go-live posts

In Livestream → Setup, open Discord and paste a webhook URL for the channel you
want. While Announce when I go live is on, every public Go Live posts one
message there under your name and avatar, with the stream title, the thumbnail
and a link button for each destination that's live. Unlisted and private
streams never post.

The post gains buttons as more destinations come up, and when you stop it turns
into "Was live" with how long you streamed. Pick No ping, @here or @everyone;
the default is No ping. You can turn off the small "Live with Videorc" footer.

## Stream Manager

The stats bar no longer shows the stream health dot and bitrate. Session time,
viewers and followers keep their places, and a failed destination still shows
in Activity.

## Windows recording

Screen-only recordings keep more frames in reserve, so a busy moment on the PC
is less likely to make the recording skip a frame.

## Known issue

Output → Local recording now has a Separate source recordings switch. It works
on Mac only for now: on Windows and Linux, leave it off, because with it on and
a camera and a screen selected, Record can't start.
