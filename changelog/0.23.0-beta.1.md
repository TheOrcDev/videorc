## New

- **Stream Manager Screen key** for the Stream Deck: tap it to move the Stream Manager between your Presenting and Monitor screens, or hold it to show the Stream Manager on the other screen until you let go. The app you're presenting keeps the focus. If your Videorc is too old for the key, it reads Update Videorc. Thanks @franky47! (#1307)
