## New

- **A stream report in your inbox**: about a minute after a live stream of 5 minutes or more, your account gets one email with the peak and average number of people watching, how many chatted, a chart of watching over time, and a table per platform (peak, average, chatted, messages, new followers). Turn reports on or off in the Go Live panel. (#1360)
- **Buddy is now Sidekick**: everywhere you read or say it, Buddy is now called Sidekick, because speech to text kept hearing "Buddy" as "body". Your settings carry over. (#1356)
- **A tidy sidebar**: the Livestream page is now **Destinations**, in a new **Livestream** group with Sidekick. ⌘1 to ⌘9 now run down the sidebar in order. (#1357)
- **A tidy Studio**: the Ready badge row above Record and Go Live is gone; the state and clock stay in the status bar, which now adds "· Rec" while you're live and recording. The button now just says "Go Live". (#1352)

## Fixes

- A background chosen for a camera-only scene now frames the camera, the way it frames a shared screen, instead of being hidden behind it. (#1353)
- With Key out background on, small leftover specks on the green screen (like a teal dot) are cleaned up instead of showing in the preview, recording and stream. (#1351)
- When the Preview is floating or closed, the Studio stage brings it back in one click with **Show here**, or opens it in its own window. (#1308)
