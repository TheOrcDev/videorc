## Fixes

- **A guest who leaves comes off the stage**: when a guest leaves, is removed or is banned, they come off the stage, the spotlight and the screens at once, so the stream no longer shows "Back in a moment" for someone who is gone. A guest whose connection only drops keeps their place until they return.
- **Phone cameras are drawn whole**: a camera held upright, such as a phone, shows the whole person over a soft blurred fill instead of a crop of the middle. The Show stage and the Program monitor show the same picture viewers get.
- **Chat and stream status come back after your phone**: after you continue a show on your phone and take it back on this Mac, Stream Manager shows the chat, the viewer count and each platform as live again, with no restart.
- **Hide screen while a room is open**: the Show stage's Screens section has Hide screen (and Show screen) with its key, so you no longer have to find it elsewhere while the room replaces the Preview.
- **Steadier after a restart**: if Videorc restarts during a show, announcements and other show changes wait until this Mac is back in control instead of being refused.
- **Your microphone survives a device change**: when macOS renumbers your audio devices, Videorc still finds the microphone you picked, by its name and identity, instead of recording without sound.
- **The guest page's theme switch**: the light and dark switch no longer shows during a call, where it did nothing. The call view stays dark.

## Improvements

- **An honest connection dot**: on a talk show, the dot next to each person in the Show stage and Guests now reflects their real connection, and turns amber when it is weak. This is the first step toward showing how much delay each guest has.
