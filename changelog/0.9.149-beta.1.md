## Live backgrounds

- Silk, Side Rays, Plasma Wave, Liquid Chrome and Line Waves now sit next to
  your pictures in Assets → Backgrounds, in the new **Live** group. Click one
  to put it behind your screen and camera; click it again to take it off.
- **Adjust style** on a live background picks one of three looks, the motion
  (Calm, the default, or Normal), how much of it shows around your screen, and
  how dark it is.
- They move in your preview, your recordings and your streams. Saved scenes
  remember the background, its look and its motion.
- They're free, and they cost nothing while you aren't using one.
- If your Mac gets too busy, Videorc draws the background at half size, then
  holds a still frame of it and tells you, so your recording keeps its frame
  rate.
- Live backgrounds move up to 4K at 30 fps. A 4K 60 fps session shows a still
  frame of yours and says so when it starts.

The five backgrounds come from React Bits by David Haz, used with his
permission.

## Faster and lighter

- The Library loads only the recordings on the page you're looking at, so it
  opens quickly however long your history is.
- Videorc no longer keeps old error pages in its app cache, and it frees the
  stale cache once. On some Macs that gives back a couple of gigabytes.
- Saving to Videorc's database is quicker, with the same safety.
- Busy chat in the Stream Manager draws once per frame instead of once per
  message.
- Captions, Buddy's bubbles and other sessions' health no longer redraw every
  Studio view.
- Videorc checks its own memory and CPU use without starting a new process
  every second.
