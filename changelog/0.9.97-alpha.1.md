## Recording on a slower PC

A tester with Intel UHD 600 graphics lost three recordings in a row. The
recording had not crashed. The PC could not keep up with the default 1440p
output, and when they pressed Stop, Videorc ended the recording before it had
finished writing.

- **Stop waits for your file.** If the PC fell behind while recording, Stop now
  gives the recording time to finish instead of cutting it short. If Videorc
  ever does have to force a stop, it says so plainly instead of reporting an
  encoder failure.
- **Resolution steps down.** When the graphics chip rejects a large output,
  Videorc now tries a smaller one on the fast hardware path, instead of
  recording at full size on a much slower path.
- **Better reports.** If a recording still fails, the support bundle now keeps
  the last lines from the encoder, so the cause can be found from one report.

## Check what your PC can record

- **Measured once, by itself.** Videorc makes a few short test recordings, from
  4K30 down to 720p30, and finds the highest output your PC holds comfortably.
  The Recording tab shows the result, marks presets as Verified or Too heavy,
  and one click applies the recommendation. Check again whenever you like.
- It uses a generated test picture, so it needs no camera, screen or microphone
  access, and the test recordings never appear in your Library.
- **A safer starting point.** New installs begin at 1080p30 until the PC has
  been measured. If you already chose an output, Videorc never changes it for
  you. You only get a warning if it measures too heavy.

## Chat, not Comments

- The detached window where you read your viewers is now called Chat
  everywhere: the window, its shortcut, the command palette and the Go Live
  dialog.
- **Put the highlight where you want it.** A new Highlight position menu in the
  Chat header lets you choose top left, top right, bottom left or bottom right,
  so a highlighted message never lands on top of your camera. Your pick is
  remembered and is used even when the Chat window is closed.
- The viewer's name now sits with their message instead of floating centred
  above it.

## New destinations and Live Co-host

- **TikTok**, **Instagram** and **YouTube Vertical** cards join the destination
  list. TikTok and Instagram use a stream key from TikTok LIVE Center or
  Instagram Live Producer. The key changes every broadcast and the card links
  you to the right place each time.
- **Instagram publishes on its side.** Start streaming in Videorc first, then
  press "Go live" in Live Producer once it shows your picture.
- Up to 5 destinations on every plan, and a failing destination only stops its
  own stream.
- **Chat rules for the co-host.** Add plain-language rules such as "no
  spoilers" in the co-host settings. A flagged message can show what kind of
  problem it is and a suggested action. Videorc never bans, deletes or times
  out anyone for you.

## Known limitations on Windows

- The glass look of the Chat and Captions windows is macOS only in this build.
- Streaming both orientations at once from one Go Live is not available on
  Windows in this build.
- Neither TikTok nor Instagram offers a public chat feed, so the Chat window
  says so instead of pretending.
- Windows DirectShow still lacks the native microphone source-loss monitor.
