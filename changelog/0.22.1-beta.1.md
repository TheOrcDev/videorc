## Fixes

- **Green screen**: the key judges each pixel together with its neighbours, so camera noise no longer leaves flickering specks ("snow") of green screen beside your head, shoulders or mic. Your outline and the colours on your clothes stay as they were, and there is no new setting. (#1299)
