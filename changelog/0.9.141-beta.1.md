## Captions that keep up

Live captions used to wait for each three-second stretch of speech before
transcribing it, so they ran a few seconds behind you. Videorc can now stream
your microphone to transcription as you speak, so words appear while you are
still saying them and a line settles moments after you pause. The same captions
reach your stream, your recording's subtitles and the captioned copy.

## Rolling out

Instant captions are turning on for Premium accounts in stages. Until they
reach yours, captions keep working the way they do today, and the Captions page
says they run in 3-second chunks. If streaming ever drops, captions fall back
to chunks on their own and keep going.
