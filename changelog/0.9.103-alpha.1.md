## Schedule X broadcasts

In Livestream → Upcoming, choose **Schedule on X** to plan a broadcast on your X
account: a title, description, thumbnail, start time, planned end, and whether
the replay stays available. When it is time, choose **Go Live…** on the
broadcast, pick your X destination, and start from Studio as usual. A scheduled
broadcast never starts on its own. Scheduling needs X Live authorized for your
account, the same as going live on X from Videorc.

Upcoming gives YouTube and X their own sections, each with its own **Schedule**
button, and every saved stream shows its state, when it airs and one main
action, with the rest in the ⋯ menu.

## A calmer desktop layout

Pages are flush panes instead of stacks of cards. Record and Stream sit right
above your session details in Studio's inspector, and a quiet status bar along
the bottom shows your session state and opens Search, Preview, Notes and Chat.
Status pills, tags and key hints are small pieces of glass that keep colour for
what matters. Settings is organised as grouped rows, and text fields get the
standard Cut, Copy and Paste menu. The Videorc icon and name lead the top of the
sidebar, like other Windows 11 apps, and Settings → About shows your version.

On Windows 11 version 22H2 or later, the main window uses the system Mica
material. Windows 10 keeps a solid background, and the Chat, Captions, Notes and
Preview windows stay solid and dark on every version.

## Change sources without stopping

Pick a different camera, microphone or display while you are recording,
streaming or both. Your recording keeps going and your stream stays connected.
Videorc shows the change as pending until the new source is really in use, and
tells you if a device could not be switched so you can retry. Switching to a
different window during a session is not available on Windows yet; choose a
display instead, or change the window before you start.

## Upcoming streams

The Upcoming workspace prepares scheduled YouTube events. Public builds cannot
connect a YouTube account while Google approval is pending. Authorized
verification builds can prepare an event with title, description, thumbnail,
time, privacy and audience setting; keep its watch link after restart; edit or
cancel it; and select it before going live. The scheduled time never starts
broadcasting automatically; you choose **Go Live** and later **Stop**.

## Save a scene and return to it

Save your sources, layout, framing, background and visual settings as a scene.
Apply it from the Scene controls or Studio gallery, including while recording.
Videorc checks that the saved sources are available before switching. Changes to
your working scene remain available after restarting the app.

## Smoother Freeform editing

Move and resize sources with smoother motion and a stable position when you let
go. Freeform starts with snapping off; turn Snap on when you want alignment
guides. Small sources are easier to grab, aspect-locked resizing is more
predictable, and the cleaner canvas controls match the rest of Videorc.

## Cleaner switching and simpler setup

- Scene switches no longer show diagnostic flashes or placeholder blocks.
- Each layout remembers its own framing when you switch away and come back.
- Search the source picker and keep the source list open while selecting sources.
- Remove a background and keep it removed after restarting.
- Configure global layout shortcuts in Settings.
- The Streaming tab's Setup and Upcoming switch marks the view you are on.

## Updates

Settings → About & updates now tells you when no Windows update is published
for you yet, and keeps checking in the background, so a newer Alpha is found
without restarting Videorc.

## Also included in this Windows test build

The layout editor supports exact position and size fields. Vertical layouts include screen Fit and Fill
controls. Co-host is now called Orcle; it still drafts chat replies
for you to approve. A takeover no longer leaves the microphone muted after a
restart, and the mixer clearly shows when the microphone is muted.

The build also includes Phone remote for chat and studio controls on your Wi-Fi,
Chat highlight positioning, a Chat window that stays usable at narrow widths
while always showing the viewer count, hardware-based recording recommendations, and better
recording finalization on slower Windows PCs. TikTok, Instagram and YouTube
Vertical destinations remain available through their supported setup paths.

Windows remains an Alpha. See the [Windows Alpha notes](https://www.videorc.com/windows-alpha)
for supported hardware and current limitations.
