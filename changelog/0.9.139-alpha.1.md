## The real X mark

Everywhere Videorc shows X, you now see X's official mark: chat and Activity
rows, filters, the status bar, Livestream setup, the highlight card on your
stream and the phone remote. It is drawn in one solid colour, black in light
mode and white in dark.

## Verified checks on X comments

When a verified X account comments, Videorc shows X's own check right after
their name: blue for X Premium, gold for businesses and gray for government
accounts. The check appears in the Stream Manager, on the highlight card and on
the phone remote.

## Room for the platform icons

The Stream Manager footer is a little taller, so the platform icons no longer
press against its border.
