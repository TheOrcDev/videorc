## A co-host that can say more

The Live Co-host is still in Alpha. This update teaches the app the richer
messages the co-host service is about to start sending. Everything below
arrives from the server side, so it lights up gradually over the coming weeks
without another download - and until then your co-host behaves exactly as it
does today.

- **Chat rules.** Settings, Live Co-host has a new "Chat rules" list: up to ten
  short plain-language rules such as "no spoilers" or "English only". You can
  write them now. The co-host starts flagging against them once the new
  moderation is switched on for your account.
- **Richer flags.** A flagged message can say what kind of problem it is, who
  it is aimed at and what you might do about it, instead of a bare warning.
- **Flag sensitivity.** Choose how sure the co-host should be before it shows
  you a flag. This filters on your Mac, so changing it is instant.
- **Suggested highlights.** Comments the co-host thinks are worth showing get a
  "Suggested" badge. Tapping it uses the same highlight you already have -
  nothing ever goes on stream by itself.
- **Chat alerts.** When several different people trip the same problem within a
  minute, the co-host pane shows a quiet chip. It never interrupts you with a
  pop-up.

## Good to know

- If the service does not speak the new format yet, the app quietly falls back
  to the current one. No pause, no error.
- The co-host still never sends anything to chat without you.
