## New

- **Go Live in polished copper**: the Studio's Go Live button, and the start buttons in the Go Live dialog, are now polished copper, so going live stands apart from Record. The button keeps one width through Go Live, Checking… and End livestream, and Checking… holds the click without dimming. (#1386)

## Fixes

- Cancelling an avatar creation stays cancelled: an older status reply no longer brings it back, leaving the creator mid-reply no longer opens another sheet, and a failed cancel keeps your "Looks right" marks. (#1380)
- The Preview's startup card gives each startup step its full time again when a step is retried, instead of showing the old warning straight away. (#1381)
- Sidekick wakes on its own name and your avatar's name. The hidden wake words kept from before the rename are retired, and a viewer whose name was one of them can be asked about like anyone else. (#1384, #1385)
