## Fixes

- **Windows microphone no longer drops out under load**: on a busy laptop, microphone buffers could reach Videorc a fraction of a second late, or arrive in a burst after a stall, and Videorc stopped the microphone, losing part of a stream or recording. Late and bursty buffers are now tolerated and the microphone keeps going. (#1525)
- **Windows Stop is steady on NVIDIA graphics**: about one Stop in thirty could sit in "Recording" for five seconds while the NVIDIA encoder finished its last frame. Videorc now collects that frame first, so Stop and the Clips-to-Record hand-over no longer wait. (#1530)
- **Windows Stop wakes the capture monitor at once**: after a Stop, Studio no longer lingers in "Stopping" and Clips re-arms as soon as the take is closed. (#1407)
- **Linux, Wayland**: the shortcut dialog no longer promises keys that work from any app. Under Wayland it now says plainly that this session cannot hear the key while Videorc is in the background. (#1529)

## Improvements

- **Windows Record starts faster**: starting a Record from Clips reuses the running capture thread instead of building a new one, which cut warm click-to-recording from about 600 ms to about 300 ms on our test machine. (#1407)
