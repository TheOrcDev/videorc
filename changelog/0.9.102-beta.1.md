## Real glass everywhere

Every Videorc window now uses the same material macOS apps use: real window
glass that picks up the colour of what sits behind it, without letting anything
behind it become readable. Chat, Captions, Notes and Preview stay dark in both
themes, because they sit next to your video. If you turn on **Reduce
Transparency** in System Settings, every window turns solid; **Increase
Contrast** makes the glass denser and the edges stronger.

## A calmer Studio

Pages are flush panes now, not stacks of cards. Record and Stream sit right
above your session details in Studio's inspector, next to the inputs they use.
A quiet status bar along the bottom shows your connection and recording state,
with your shortcuts one click away.

## Glass badges

Status pills, tags and key hints are small pieces of glass. Text stays neutral;
colour appears only where it carries meaning: a green dot when something is
healthy, amber when it needs a look, and red when you are on air or something
failed.

## Native touches

Settings is organised as grouped rows, like System Settings. Tabs are a glass
segmented control that always marks the view you are on, including Setup and
Upcoming in the Streaming tab. Text fields get the standard Cut, Copy and Paste
menu with spelling suggestions. Scrollbars are the macOS overlay scrollbars.
Buttons and labels no longer select like text on a web page.
