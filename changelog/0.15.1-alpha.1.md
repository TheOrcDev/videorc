This is the next Windows and Linux Alpha after 0.15.0-alpha.1.

## Studio

- Vertical 9:16 keeps the size and frame rate you chose. 720p now records at
  720 by 1280, 1440p at 1440 by 2560 and 4K at 2160 by 3840, instead of always
  going to 1080 by 1920. Leaving vertical brings your landscape size back.

## Look

- The Liquid Chrome ground drifts slightly in the window you are looking at,
  so the app feels alive without pulling your eye.

## Library

- A take that Videorc was stopped in the middle of, from a crash, a killed app
  or a power cut, is finished into an MP4 with a poster the next time Videorc
  starts. The Library marks it Recovered, and the original recording is always
  kept.

## Linux

- Videorc learns that a newer version is on videorc.com and says so.
- Clips waits for the screen share to be chosen instead of showing an error
  when Videorc launches.
