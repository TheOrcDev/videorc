## Named markers

While you record or stream, mark where a new topic starts. Say "Orcle, make a
marker here for Shadcn New Library", or type `/marker Shadcn New Library` in
the Stream Manager. You get a confirmation with Undo.

In the Library, open a session's markers to jump to that moment in the video,
rename a marker or delete it. Streams without a recording keep the marker times
too.

## Watch streaks

Twitch watch streaks now appear in the Stream Manager's Activity, with the
viewer's message and how many streams in a row they watched. Thank them in chat
or show the streak on stream in one click.

## Links in chat

Right-click a link in chat to open it in your browser or copy it.
