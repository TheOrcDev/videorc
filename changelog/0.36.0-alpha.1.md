## New

- **Live captions in one switch**: turning on Live captions in the Captions tab puts captions on the stream, in every scene. A new Show captions on choice picks Live stream only (the default) or Live stream and recording, so the recording can get a copy with captions too. The Scene page now only places the caption bar.
- **An Assets group in the sidebar**: the Assets page splits into Images and Sounds (the soundboard), under a new Assets group below Livestream. Images is Ctrl+Shift+G and Sounds is Ctrl+Shift+S. Library moves down beside Settings on Ctrl+0, and the pages above run on Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9.
- **Discord gets its own tab**: Destinations → Discord holds your webhooks, Announce when I go live, Post as, Ping, the Videorc footer and Links in the post. The "Couldn't post to Discord" notice opens it directly.

## Improvements

- **Hide screen looks designed**: a hidden screen shows one opaque still, a calm gradient with a glass eye-slash, instead of a blur made from your screen. The recording, every stream, Clips and the Preview all show the same still.
- **A simpler Destinations page**: the Technical details section is gone. The Stream settings row in the checklist shows the output check, and Health → Recording and encoder now shows the stream frame rate and bitrate target.

## Fixes

- **Stream quality options stay in their track**: on Destinations → Output quality, a Stream quality choice that wraps to a second row no longer lies over the description under it.
- **Hide screen without a black flash on Windows**: on the D3D11 path, Hide screen no longer puts black frames on the recording, the stream or Clips. Videorc holds the last frame for up to 300 ms until the still is ready.
- **A quick Stop on NVIDIA**: on the CPU path, Stop no longer waits 5 seconds on the NVIDIA encoder, so a take no longer ends as recovery media and Record starts right away after Clips.
