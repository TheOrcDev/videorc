This is the next Windows and Linux Alpha after 0.11.0-alpha.1.

## 1-minute clips

Clips now saves 30 seconds or 1 minute, chosen when you press. The Stream
Manager status bar shows **Clip 30 s** and **1 min** side by side, Settings →
Shortcuts has a second shortcut for the 1-minute clip, and `/clip 60` or
"clip the last minute" saves 1 minute. The Output → Clips length row is gone;
a different length you had set goes back to 30 seconds. A 1-minute clip keeps
about twice as much on disk while Clips is on.

## Windows

Recording now falls back when the raw pipe fails, finishes cleanly when the
encoder stops with an error, and gives a software encoder more time to start.
Thanks to Pete for the original fix.

## Linux

The source pickers stay unlocked while Clips runs, and Pulse audio no longer
dies when the system clock jumps.

## Fixes

- Clips comes back after its buffer stops, and that is no longer reported as a
  failed recording.
- Idle soundboard pad presses no longer end up in your clips.
- With separate recordings, the Screen file survives a stale recovery latch,
  and Clips stops looping.
- A scene that shows a source you turned off now waits with a plain message.
- "Recording stopped unexpectedly" and "Livestream stopped unexpectedly" now
  have a Copy button.
