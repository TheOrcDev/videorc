## Fixes

- Scrollbars no longer draw a gray track: with a mouse connected, only a thin thumb shows, and trackpad scrollbars still fade away after you scroll. (#1390)
- The copper Go Live button no longer runs past the inspector's right edge: Record and Go Live now share the row in two equal halves. (#1390)
