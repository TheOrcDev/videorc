## New

- **Chat commands**: viewers type `!go` in chat and Videorc answers as you, with the reply you saved, starting with their @name, in the chat they asked in. `!commands` lists every command. Set up in Setup → Livestream → Chat commands. Free for everyone. (#1278)
- Stream Deck keys show what is live, plus a Buddy Live key, from François Best. A paired phone can turn Buddy Live on or off too. (#1270)

## Fixes

- A layout missing its screen or camera says what to pick, not "try again". (#1276)
- Going live no longer freezes for ten seconds at the start. (#1192)

## Windows

- A Windows camera runs in its own best mode at its native size, and Health names a slow one. (#1277)
- Windows recordings keep their frames when the encoder pauses, finish when it sticks, and Hide screen stays frosted through Show. Hybrid-graphics laptops can record. (#1262, #1260, #1259, #1257, #1184, #1126)
- Windows cameras open on the right device, start without holding up Record and Go Live, and Record keeps the Clips microphone open. (#1229, #1235, #1241, #1228)

## Linux

- After you stop a Linux recording, the live background on the idle Preview is current again. (#1258)
