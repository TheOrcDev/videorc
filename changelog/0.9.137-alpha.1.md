This is the next Windows and Linux Alpha after 0.9.136-alpha.1.

## Twitch Power-ups and channel points

Bits spent on Power-ups (Celebrations, gigantified emotes, message effects and
your own Custom Power-ups) now appear in the Stream Manager's Activity under
**Tips**, and count toward the stream's bits total. Channel point redemptions
appear under a new **Rewards** chip, using your channel's own points name.

With Auto-show on, both pop onto your stream as the highlight card. Reconnect
Twitch once to grant the two new read permissions; Activity offers a one-click
reconnect while they're missing.

## A dropped destination comes back

When a platform closed its end of a stream mid-broadcast, that destination
stayed down while Videorc kept saying On air. Now each destination retries on
its own and resumes on the next keyframe, and the app says which destination
is reconnecting or isn't being received.
