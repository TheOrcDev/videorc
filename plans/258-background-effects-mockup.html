Background effects: where the control lives

Blur or replace what is behind you without a green screen. One Background control sits where Green screen sits today: a line on the Studio's Camera row, a section in the Scene page's camera inspector, and a round button on the guest page.

1 Background choice 0 raw frames out Dark and light

1. Studio: the Camera row's Background line

The line under the Camera picker that holds Green screen today (plan 248) becomes Background. One click opens the picker: a live "What viewers see" picture over the choices. A pick applies at once and the stage confirms it, so no toast.

2. Scene page: the camera's Background section (the home)

The camera inspector's Green screen section becomes Background, in the same place, right under the camera's name. The tuning for the pick folds open under the tiles: Blur strength for a blur, Fit and Choose image… for a picture, and today's Similarity, Smoothness, Spill removal and Hide edges for a green or blue screen.

3. When it can't keep up, and when macOS already does it

The two lines the Camera row adds under Background. Neither toasts. While the effect is behind, viewers see the whole camera blurred (or the picture alone), never the room.

4. Guest page: the green room

A round Background button joins the camera's controls on the preview, between Camera and Devices. Its popover shows the same blur and picture choices; the preview is what the host and viewers will see. The effect runs in the guest's browser before anything is sent.

5. Guest page: in the call

The same round button sits in the call's control bar after Camera, so a guest can change it mid-show. The guest's own tile shows the processed picture, the one that is sent.
The pictures are schematic. Tile names stand for the bundled images and loops; "Show set" is the host's own scene background, offered to guests so a talk show looks like one set.
