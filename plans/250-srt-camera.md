## The ask (2026-11-13)

A clean, high-resolution picture from a camera that sends its video over SRT, taken as a camera in Videorc, "minimal on the Videorc side".

So Videorc gets a **generic SRT input**. Videorc has no knowledge of any sender, and any SRT sender works: a camera app, a hardware encoder, streaming software.

The numbers below were measured with a real sender over a USB network link: HEVC Main 10 at 4K25 or 1080p30, steady, 0 packets lost, 0.36–0.49 s from glass to arrival with a 40 ms SRT buffer.

## What a streamer gets

1. In Settings → General there is an **SRT input** switch with a **Beta** badge: "Adds the SRT Camera to the camera list in Sources." It is off by default.
2. When it is on, a camera named **SRT Camera (port 9000)** appears in the camera list. The streamer picks it in Sources → Video → Camera like any camera.
3. While the SRT Camera is the picked camera, its row in Sources shows, under the facts line:
   - **Port** (default 9000);
   - **Passphrase**, generated by Videorc, with **Copy** and **New passphrase**;
   - **Connection**: "Cable or Ethernet · 0 ms", "Wi-Fi · 120 ms" (default), "Internet · 500 ms";
   - the lip-sync note.
4. While a scene uses it, Videorc listens on the port. When no scene uses it, the port is closed.
5. Until a sender connects, the camera shows a still: "Waiting for SRT stream on port 9000". When frames arrive, the picture replaces the still.
6. If the stream drops, the last frame holds for 2 s, then the still comes back. The picture returns by itself when frames arrive again.
7. If the SRT Camera is in the scene with no signal, **Go live** asks first.
8. Lip sync: the streamer uses the existing microphone sync offset in the audio mixer (measured by hand for each sender).

## What exists today (verified at `f3cf8799b`)

- **Camera list:** `list_native_cameras` lists AVFoundation devices ([camera_capture.rs:695-723](../crates/videorc-backend/src/camera_capture.rs)). Ids are `camera:avfoundation-native:<hexadecimal id>` (`camera_capture.rs:7`).
- **Cameras that are not AVFoundation already exist:** the Linux v4l2 and Windows dshow pipe cameras publish into the same `PreviewCameraShared` frame store and count callbacks the same way ([preview_camera.rs:676-687](../crates/videorc-backend/src/preview_camera.rs)). The SRT Camera follows that model.
- **Frame store:** BGRA only (`PreviewCameraPixelFormat::Bgra8`, `preview_camera.rs:543-546`). A BGRA `CVPixelBuffer` goes in without a copy through `RetainedPixelBuffer` and `publish_with_source_handles` (`preview_camera.rs:6920-7014`, `frame_store.rs`). The Metal compositor imports it through a `CVMetalTextureCache` (`metal_compositor.rs:2016-2075`).
- **Scenes:** a camera is `SceneSourceKind::Camera` (`protocol.rs:1029-1034`). The SRT Camera adds no source kind.
- **Camera failure paths that must not fire on a silent SRT Camera:**
  - the 15 s warm-start budget for slow cameras ([live_layout.rs:65-71](../crates/videorc-backend/src/live_layout.rs));
  - the adaptive step-down to 1080p ([camera_capture.rs:9-17](../crates/videorc-backend/src/camera_capture.rs));
  - camera capture recovery.
- **Decoding:** `objc2-video-toolbox` enables only compression features today ([Cargo.toml:72](../crates/videorc-backend/Cargo.toml)). The macOS FFmpeg build has no libsrt ([scripts/build-ffmpeg-macos.sh:70-93](../scripts/build-ffmpeg-macos.sh)).
- **Microphone sync offset:** a manual trim from −1000 to +1000 ms in the audio mixer (`MICROPHONE_SYNC_OFFSET_MIN_MS` / `MAX_MS`, `apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/lib/capture.ts:682-683`). No change.
- **Beta badge precedent:** the Quick Sync encoder option (`apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/components/settings/encoder-preference-field.tsx:43`).
- **Go live asks:** the Unlisted or Private ask from 0.24.0 (#1316, `go-live-flow.ts`) is the pattern for D12.
- **Camera permission:** the SRT Camera needs no camera TCC grant, so the dev app can test it. Today a real-camera check needs the packaged app (AGENTS.md).

## Decisions (2026-11-13)

- **D1. Generic, one-way.** Videorc has no knowledge of any sender. No sender's name, protocol or API appears in Videorc's code, copy or docs. A sender may know how Videorc works; Videorc never depends on a sender.
- **D2. Passive receiver.** Videorc only listens. It never starts, stops or configures a sender. Opening the listener is the only signal a sender can see.
- **D3. An opt-in setting, a camera entry.** One "SRT input" switch in Settings → General, off by default. When it is on, the camera list shows **SRT Camera (port {PORT})**. The streamer picks it like any camera. Its port, passphrase and Connection sit on the Camera row in Sources while it is the picked camera: Sources is the home of every camera, so they are not mirrored in Settings (option B, 2026-11-14). Videorc listens only while a scene uses it.
- **D4. Passphrase by default.** Videorc generates a random passphrase (SRT allows 10 to 79 characters; use 32 URL-safe ones). It shows the passphrase with Copy and New passphrase. It refuses every sender without the passphrase, and the stream is encrypted. A sender gets the passphrase by copy and paste. There is no setting for "no passphrase".
- **D5. Lip sync by the existing microphone offset.** No new audio code. The note in the setting says: "This camera arrives late. Set the microphone sync offset in the audio mixer to match."
- **D6. The sender picks the format.** Videorc takes whatever resolution and frame rate arrive and scales them to the canvas, as for any camera. No size or rate setting. At 25 fps on a 30 or 60 fps canvas, frames repeat. That is accepted.
- **D7. No signal is a calm state.** While no frame has arrived, the camera shows a still with "Waiting for SRT stream on port {PORT}". It is not a failed camera: no warm-start time-out, no recovery, no step-down, no error toast. The picture replaces the still on the first decoded frame.
- **D8. A drop holds 2 s.** On a gap in frames, hold the last frame for 2 s, then show the waiting still. Recover by itself when frames arrive.
- **D9. macOS first.** The setting appears only on macOS in this plan. Keep the parts that differ per platform (decode, and the handoff to the frame store) behind one small boundary. The setting, camera entry, listener, demuxer, still, hold and Go live ask are shared code. Windows and Linux are a later plan, and can feed the frame store through the existing FFmpeg pipe camera path.
- **D10. Video only.** Ignore the audio in the stream. Note the gap in the docs as a follow-up for other senders.
- **D11. Connection picker, not a number.** The SRT receive latency comes from **Connection**: Cable or Ethernet = 0 ms (40 ms until 2026-11-18: a sender on a USB link measured no loss at 0, and 0 saves about 26 ms), Wi-Fi = 120 ms (default), Internet = 500 ms. Show the ms in each label. No free number field. SRT uses the larger of the two ends' values, so Videorc's value is a floor for the sender.
- **D12. HEVC and H.264.** Decode both with VideoToolbox, including HEVC Main 10. Any other codec shows "This SRT stream uses a codec Videorc can't show ({codec})" on the still. No FFmpeg in this camera path.
- **D13. Beta.** The setting carries a "Beta" badge, like the Quick Sync option. It loses the badge once a second, independent sender works.
- **D14. One sender at a time.** The first sender to connect keeps the camera, and a second one is refused. When the sender leaves, the next one to connect takes over. A sender that connects and leaves without data (a pairing check) must not stall or break the camera.
- **D15. Go live asks with no signal.** If a scene that goes live uses the SRT Camera and it has no signal, **Go live** asks first. Use the pattern of the Unlisted ask (#1316). Recording does not ask. An agent's `go_live` (Buddy too) has no dialog to ask in: it is refused with the dialog's sentence unless the call passes `allowNoSignal`, the way `allowNonPublic` answers the Unlisted ask (2026-11-14).
- **D16. Listen only while used.** The port is open only while the setting is on and a scene in use contains the SRT Camera. Bind all interfaces, because a sender can arrive on any of them, for example a USB network link. The passphrase is the guard.
- **D17. Status in the camera's details.** Show the state in the camera's detail line: "Waiting", "Receiving · 3840×2160 · 25 fps · HEVC", "Sender refused: wrong passphrase" (if SRT reports it), "Second sender refused". No other new UI.
- **D18. Self-healing receiver** (2026-11-14). Resilience is a primary goal: if the feed drops during a stream, the SRT Camera heals by itself as soon as a sender connects again, with no click in Videorc. Videorc stays a passive listener (D2) and the sender redials; Videorc is never the reason a redial fails.
  1. The listener never stays dead while the camera is in use: any listener or accept error restarts it with a short backoff (capped at 2 s), the detail line says so, and the camera and the scene never stop.
  2. A sender that redials gets in at once: the current sender is released as soon as libsrt reports its socket broken or closed; a new connection from the current sender's own IP address replaces it at once (a generic receiver rule); other addresses keep the 2 s silent rule.
  3. A second sender is refused with `SRT_REJX_OVERLOAD` (1402, "busy"), so a sender can tell busy from a wrong passphrase (`SRT_REJ_BADSECRET`, 10, set by libsrt). Both are documented in `docs/srt-camera.md` as the receiver's contract. (The brief first said 1503; that is `SRT_REJX_DOWN`. 1402 was confirmed on 2026-11-14.)
  4. The decoder heals: a VideoToolbox error, an invalid session (after a sleep) or new parameter sets (a resolution or rate change) rebuild the session from the next key frame; frames arriving with none decoded for 2 s show the still with the status.
  5. The output never breaks: a drop during a recording or a livestream holds, then shows the still, then the picture again.

## SRT library (chosen at slicing, 2026-11-13)

**libsrt 1.5.7 (Haivision, MPL-2.0), loaded at run time through a small FFI.**

- Rejected: the pure-Rust `srt-tokio` / `srt-protocol` 0.4.4 (Apache-2.0).
  A listener with a passphrase panics (`unimplemented!("Key size mismatch")`,
  `unimplemented!("expected no secrets")`) on handshake fields the sender
  picks, so any sender on the network could kill the listener task. Its
  last release was 2024-05. Tested here against Homebrew `srt-live-transmit`
  1.5.7: the first caller with a passphrase killed the listener.
- Rejected: a `-sys` crate that builds libsrt in `build.rs`. It would put
  CMake and OpenSSL into every `cargo build` and `cargo test`, on CI and on
  Windows and Linux, for a macOS-only Beta.
- Chosen: `scripts/build-libsrt-macos.sh` builds `libsrt.dylib` from the
  pinned source archive (SHA-256 checked), with Homebrew's OpenSSL 3 linked
  **statically**, the same way `scripts/build-ffmpeg-macos.sh` builds the
  FFmpeg bundle. It stages `vendor/libsrt/current/` (git-ignored) with the
  licence text and source notes. electron-builder ships it as an extra
  resource next to FFmpeg; main passes its path to the backend in
  `VIDEORC_BUNDLED_LIBSRT_PATH`; the backend `dlopen`s it the first time an
  SRT Camera starts. Cargo, CI and the other platforms never see it.
- Licence: MPL-2.0 is file-level copyleft and allows a larger work under the
  AGPL-3.0 (MPL-2.0 §3.3, "Secondary License"; libsrt's files carry no
  "Incompatible With Secondary Licenses" notice). Videorc ships libsrt's
  source files unmodified, so the duty is to keep the MPL notice and say
  where the source is: `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and the staged
  `vendor/libsrt/current/licenses/`. OpenSSL 3 is Apache-2.0, already in the
  FFmpeg bundle on the same terms.
- In a dev build with no staged library the backend also tries Homebrew's
  `libsrt.dylib`, the way macOS dev resolves FFmpeg from `PATH`. A packaged
  build never does.

## Slices

Order: S1 → S2 → S3 → S4 → S5 → S6 → S7. Each slice leaves the tree green.

### S1: The setting and the camera entry (backend)

`srt_camera/settings.rs` (new), `srt_camera/mod.rs` (new), `protocol.rs`,
`main.rs`, `devices.rs`, `backend_authority.rs` (no change: renderer role).

- `SrtInputSettings { enabled: false, port: 9000, connection: WiFi }` in
  `app_settings` under `srtInput`; the passphrase in the secret store
  (`srt-input:passphrase`), 32 URL-safe characters, made on first read,
  renewed by `srtInput.renewPassphrase`.
- `Connection` → receive latency: Cable or Ethernet 0 ms, Wi-Fi 120 ms,
  Internet 500 ms.
- RPCs `srtInput.get`, `srtInput.set`, `srtInput.renewPassphrase`; event
  `srtInput.changed`. `supported` is false off macOS (D9), and `set` refuses
  there.
- `camera:srt-input` with the name "SRT Camera (port N)" in the device list,
  only on macOS and only while the setting is on.

Done when `cargo test -p videorc-backend srt_` covers the defaults, port
bounds, the passphrase made once, kept and renewed (32 URL-safe characters),
the Connection mapping, the entry present only when on, and a WebSocket
round trip of the three RPCs.

### S2: The TS demuxer (shared)

`srt_camera/ts_demux.rs` (new), `srt_camera/annexb.rs` (new).

- PAT → PMT → the first video PID (stream type 0x1B H.264, 0x24 HEVC; any
  other video type is reported by name for the codec still).
- PES reassembly across packets, PTS read and unwrapped across the 33-bit
  wrap, adaptation fields skipped, a continuity-counter gap drops the
  partial PES, every other PID ignored (D10).
- Annex B split into NAL units; parameter sets (SPS/PPS, VPS/SPS/PPS) and
  key frames found; width and height read from the SPS for the status line.

Done when unit tests cover PAT/PMT, a PES split across packets, a PTS wrap,
a discontinuity, an audio PID ignored, both codecs' parameter sets, and an
unknown codec named.

### S3: Signal, hold and one sender (shared)

`srt_camera/signal.rs` (new).

- Frames are timed by their arrival (the sender's PTS is never used for
  presentation; doc §10).
- A pure state machine with an injected clock: Waiting → Receiving; a gap
  holds the last frame for 2 s (D8), then Waiting again; recovery on the
  next frame; a codec still for an unknown codec (D12).
- The sender gate (D14): the first sender keeps the camera; a second is
  refused and reported; a sender that leaves frees it; a sender that
  connects and leaves without data (a pairing check) frees it at once.
- The detail line (D17): "Waiting", "Receiving · 3840 × 2160 · 25 fps · HEVC",
  "Sender refused: wrong passphrase", "Second sender refused".

Done when unit tests cover each transition, the 2 s edge exactly, the fps
measured from arrivals, the second sender, the pairing check and every
detail string.

### S4: libsrt and the listener (shared, library macOS-packaged)

`scripts/build-libsrt-macos.sh` (new), `vendor/libsrt/README.md` (new),
`.gitignore`, `package.json` (`libsrt:build:macos`),
`apps/desktop/electron-builder.yml`, `apps/desktop/src/main/index.ts`
(`VIDEORC_BUNDLED_LIBSRT_PATH`), `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`,
`srt_camera/libsrt.rs` (new FFI), `srt_camera/listener.rs` (new).

- The listener binds `0.0.0.0:<port>` (D16), sets the passphrase, enforced
  encryption, live mode and the receive latency. libsrt runs its listen
  callback before it checks the passphrase, so the callback only refuses a
  busy camera, and the sender is decided at `accept` (D14; changed
  2026-11-18). libsrt says a passphrase refusal only in its log
  (`rsp(REJECT): 1010` / `1011`), so a log handler counts those and the line
  says "Sender refused: wrong passphrase".
- Receive with a short timeout so stop and the hold run on time.

Done when the option list is a pure function with a test, `cargo test`
passes without libsrt on the machine, and the build script stages a dylib
that `otool -L` shows free of Homebrew paths.

### S5: Decode and the camera (macOS boundary + wiring)

`srt_camera/apple_decode.rs` (new, the D9 boundary), `srt_camera/still.rs`
(new), `srt_camera/camera.rs` (new), `preview_camera.rs`,
`camera_capture.rs`, `recording.rs`, `Cargo.toml` (`objc2-video-toolbox`

- VideoToolbox decodes H.264 and HEVC (Main 10 included) to IOSurface BGRA

- The still: dark, centred text drawn with `ab_glyph` from a system font
  (no font file shipped); a plain still when no font is found.
- One pacer publishes into the camera frame store at the canvas rate, the
  newest frame or the still, so the camera looks healthy to every cadence

- `camera:srt-input` starts this camera instead of AVFoundation, needs no
  camera grant, and the listener lives exactly as long as the camera session

- Status changes ride `srtInput.changed`.

Done when unit tests cover the still's size and pixels, the pacer choosing
frame or still, the step-down guard, and the camera id parse; and the
fixture smoke (S7) shows a picture.

## As built

Built 2026-11-13/14 on `feat/srt-camera` (not committed). All in one pass;
the slices above held, with the changes noted here.

- **S1 setting**: `srt_camera/settings.rs` (defaults, port 1024–65535,
  Connection → 40/120/500 ms, passphrase made, kept and renewed through a
  `PassphraseVault`; the app's vault is the secret store), `srt_camera/mod.rs`
  (the process-wide service, `srtInput.get/set/renewPassphrase` logic, the

  nothing for the 2 s hold no longer keeps the camera (`SenderGate`,
  `Acceptance::Replaced`). Found by the smoke: a sender killed without a
  goodbye held the camera for SRT's 5 s idle timeout, so the next sender was
  refused as a second sender, against D14's "the next one to connect takes
  over".
- **S4 libsrt**: `scripts/build-libsrt-macos.sh`, `srt_camera/libsrt.rs`
  (`dlopen`), `srt_camera/listener.rs`; packaging in `electron-builder.yml`,
  `main/index.ts` (`VIDEORC_BUNDLED_LIBSRT_PATH`), `package.json`

  `release-build-macos.mjs`, `lab-candidate-macos.yml`,
  `preflight-macos-package.mjs`, `sign-macos-local-app.mjs`, the D3
  sensitive list in `capture-decay-release-acceptance.mjs`; notices in
  `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and `licenses/libsrt/`. Change: this first
  read, that libsrt refuses a wrong passphrase before the listen callback,
