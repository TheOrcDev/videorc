## Scope and source

Since macOS 0.9.121 (`18b8c5d522b2c8706ed3c81d3a9003ef5f4c84cb`):

- **#495, live mic loss and own-stream echo (plan 076).** The session audio
  bus no longer retires a healthy microphone when FFmpeg stops draining the
  audio FIFO while its video input is late (the owner's 0.9.120 live stream
  lost its microphone for good). Placement losses report, re-anchor and
  recover; `audio-output-stalled` reports an audible gap. The System audio
  echo guard pauses System audio when the stream's own audio comes back
  through it (a Twitch tab 3.16 s behind), with Resume and a Sources switch.
  `smoke:output-stall` passes on the branch and reproduces the incident on
  main. Owner acceptance on a packaged build and a quiet-machine
  `smoke:system-audio` are owed.
- **#494, 4K live streaming free on every plan (plan 075).** Basic gets the
  full streaming ceiling (YouTube 4K30, 1080p60); Premium keeps cloud AI,
  live captions, Orcle and Noise Cleanup. Its macOS
  `smoke:recording-matrix`/`smoke:multistream` and the videorc-web copy
  slices are owed.
- #497 (bump). The unpublished `0.9.121-alpha.1` entry moved to
  `0.9.122-alpha.1`, following the #485 and #492 precedent.

## Owner decisions (2026-09-29)

- "Merge", then "Make a release on all three platforms when merged", then
  "let's make a release for mac". #495 was merged on local evidence while its
  third CI run was in progress (the first two failed on a test-harness timing
  race, fixed in the branch). macOS follows the regular Beta scope (build,
  sign, notarize, staple, artifact validation; device, endurance and
  clean-machine gates not run).
