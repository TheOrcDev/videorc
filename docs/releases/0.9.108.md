## Scope and source

Since macOS 0.9.107 (`e3f4acb9fcfbe52a97ae98887a0871aed2c03cee`), protected
main gained:

- **#414**, the 0.9.106 week note moved into 0.9.107 (changelog only; it was
  published with 0.9.107).
- **#415, the Stream Manager puts chat first (plan 057).** The 128 px grid of
  stat tiles became one 32 px bar at every width. The clock, viewers and
  stream health lead it and are never clipped (health was missing below
  640 px). Health shows the bitrate when fine and names the problem
  otherwise. The other stats wrap out of sight whole when the window is
  narrow. Stats can be dragged into another order or hidden from a
  right-click menu; the clock and the viewer count stay in the first three
  slots. Text went quiet when fine: exception-only status and composer
  notes, hover-only chat times, one-line Activity facts (the stream's
  highlight card keeps the full sentence). Carried fixes: a pinned chat
  follows resizes, and Orcle's toast says Ctrl+J on Windows.
- **This PR:** the live chip reads ON AIR (owner call, 2026-09-24).

`0.9.107-alpha.1` was superseded before signing (see
[the 0.9.107 record](0.9.107.md)), so its unpublished changelog entry moves
forward as `0.9.108-alpha.1`, with the Stream Manager change added.
