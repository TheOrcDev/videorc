# Videorc 0.31.1 release

## Scope and source

0.31.1 is a **PATCH** hot fix from 0.31.0 (record #1503), three PRs on main:
#1504 (the Sources camera and microphone open by Chromium's decorated device
labels), #1505 (Program preview fits narrow columns; avatar and graphics
switches move into Guests > Room) and #1506 (Guests becomes its own page under
Livestream). The announced, user-facing changes are limited to the device
selection fix, preview layout fit and Livestream navigation polish. The
surfaces behind #1505 and #1506 stay **unannounced** (switches stay off) and are
not described further in the public changelog or the Discord post.
